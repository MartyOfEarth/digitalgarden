Far from bustling cities, amid the trees of trackless forests and across wide plains, [[Classes/Ranger\|Rangers]] keep their unending watch in the wilderness. [[Classes/Ranger\|Rangers]] learn to track their quarry as a predator does, moving stealthily through the wilds and hiding themselves in brush and rubble.

Thanks to their connection with nature, [[Classes/Ranger\|Rangers]] can also cast spells that harness primal powers of the wilderness. A [[Classes/Ranger\|Ranger’s]] talents and magic are honed with deadly focus to protect the world from the ravages of monsters and tyrants.
