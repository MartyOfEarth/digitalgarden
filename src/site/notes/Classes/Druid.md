[[Classes/Druid\|Druids]] belong to ancient orders that call on the forces of nature. Harnessing the magic of animals, plants, and the four elements, [[Classes/Druid\|Druids]] heal, transform into animals, and wield elemental destruction.

Revering nature above all, individual [[Classes/Druid\|Druids]] gain their magic from nature, a nature deity, or both, and they typically unite with other [[Classes/Druid\|Druids]] to perform rites that mark the passage of the seasons and other natural cycles.

[[Classes/Druid\|Druids]] are concerned with the delicate ecological balance that sustains plant and animal life and with the need for people to live in harmony with nature. [[Classes/Druid\|Druids]] often guard sacred sites or watch over regions of unspoiled nature, but when a significant danger arises, [[Classes/Druid\|Druids]] take a more active role as adventurers who combat the threat.
