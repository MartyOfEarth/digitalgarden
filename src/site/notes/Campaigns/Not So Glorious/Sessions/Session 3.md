The [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/Party/Party\|Party]] fights the [[DnD/Races/Goblin\|goblins]] and [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/Party/Valentin d’Étoile\|Valentin]] scares the horses away and interrogates one of the [[DnD/Races/Goblin\|goblins]]. Then they search the camp and find a couple of things: "G+G" in a heart drawn on a tree stamp next to a campfire, diary that says *"Dear diary throgg is evil, very bad, I will hug bug under pug, throgg will like ogboor, Dear diary i put something under wrong pillow, i feel bad, TV good friend, I will make it up tomorrow, Dear diary, run, cave something."*, and a map that leads to a lake.

[[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/NPCs/Rakk\|Rakk]] informs the [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/Party/Party\|Party]] that there are trees in this forest and then feels insulted and leaves home.

The [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/Party/Party\|Party]] heads to the lake mentioned in the map. They take boats across the lake and continue south. They get lost but with the help of some local wolves they find their way to a waterfall behind which is the goblin cave. [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/Party/Professor Nerb\|Professor Nerb]] and [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/Party/Valentin d’Étoile\|Valentin]] disguised as a [[DnD/Races/Goblin\|Goblin]] named Gabby infiltrate the cave. They tell them about the attack and start making bombs in an attempt to blow up the cave, while saying it's to destroy [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Places/Settlements/Westwend\|Westwend]]. [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/Party/Kóokay\|Kóokay]], [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/Party/Ravrokk\|Ravrokk]], and [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/NPCs/Ollitur\|Ollitur]] find a goblin named Gebby and take him hostage, then they go inside where they get another two hostages.
All of them meet and try to fight [[DnD/Races/Goblin\|goblins]] in the kitchen.

[[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/NPCs/Ollitur\|Ollitur]] forces [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/Party/Valentin d’Étoile\|Valentin]] to kill one of the [[DnD/Races/Goblin\|goblins]] which makes him go little crazy and comes to the [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/Party/Party\|Party]] with an idea to blow up the whole cave.
