[[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/Party/Ravrokk\|Ravrokk]], hired by a [[DnD/Races/Tiefling\|Tiefling]] working for [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/NPCs/Melyre\|Melyre]] to guard the door of [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Places/Settlements/Yelkwich/Inner Yelkwich/The Leaky Tap\|The Leaky Tap]] while [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/NPCs/Melyre\|Melyre]] planned a revenge attack on [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/Party/Kóokay\|Kóokay]] after she refused to join his gang, comes to the tavern. Something seems off to him, and when he arrives he finds [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/Party/Kóokay\|Kóokay]] fighting a bunch of Orcs led by [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/NPCs/Melyre\|Melyre]] and he decides to join the fight on [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/Party/Kóokay\|Kóokay's]] side. Meanwhile, [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/Party/Valentin d’Étoile\|Valentin]] is performing on the stage and decides to join in order to protect [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/Party/Kóokay\|Kóokay]] from these sketchy men.

The whole fight ends when a bunch of guards arrive and Valentin, along with one of the [[DnD/Races/Orc\|orcs]] he charmed after piercing his ear with a dagger, go voluntarily to explain the situation. He doesn't do the best job though. Nervous about the [[DnD/Races/Orc\|orc]] coming back to his senses from the Charm Person spell, [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/Party/Valentin d’Étoile\|Valentin]] makes yells and shout trying to explain what has happened. [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/Party/Ravrokk\|Ravrokk]] and [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/Party/Kóokay\|Kóokay]] try to not let one of the bandits speak, while closed in a cell, and later they get to explain their POV to the guards.

In the morning [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/Party/Kóokay\|Kóokay]] and [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/Party/Valentin d’Étoile\|Valentin]] are free to go, while [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/Party/Ravrokk\|Ravrokk]] gets bailed out. They receive [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Items/Q's Scroll\|Q's Scroll]] inviting them to come over with the promise of money. [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/Party/Ravrokk\|Ravrokk]] marches forward with [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/Party/Kóokay\|Kóokay]] following and dragging [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/Party/Valentin d’Étoile\|Valentin]] with her.

They got to [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Places/Settlements/Yelkwich/Outer Yelkwich/Qwent's Lair\|Qwent's Lair]] where they met this desperate, yet happy man - [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/NPCs/Qwent\|Qwent]]. He sent them on a quest to save [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Places/Settlements/Westwend\|Westwend]] from ongoing [[DnD/Races/Goblin\|goblin]] attacks for some coins. He also promised to pay for [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/Other/Cesario\|Cesario's]] stable in the meantime. The [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/Party/Party\|Party]] accepted his quest and went to the [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Places/Settlements/Yelkwich/Outer Yelkwich/Docks\|Docks]] where they boarded a ship manned by older [[DnD/Races/Kenku\|Kenku]] [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/NPCs/Kenny\|Kenny]]. [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/Party/Kóokay\|Kóokay]] helped him while [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/Party/Valentin d’Étoile\|Valentin]] played his lute on the bow and [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/Party/Ravrokk\|Ravrokk]] minded his business.
