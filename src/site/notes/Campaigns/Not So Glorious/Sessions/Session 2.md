The [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/Party/Party\|Party]] finds [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/Party/Professor Nerb\|Professor Nerb]] hiding in the ship and he joins them on the adventure. They continue for a couple of days while [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/Party/Kóokay\|Kóokay]] sometimes helps [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/NPCs/Kenny\|Kenny]] steer the ship, until one day [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/Party/Valentin d’Étoile\|Valentin]] spots a village on the horizon. As they get closer, it seems the dock is concerningly empty.

When they arrive, [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/Party/Valentin d’Étoile\|Valentin]] approaches a couple sitting on a bench, knitting. He asks about the situation, although they discriminate him because of his status and look. They try to swindle some money out of him, when [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/Party/Professor Nerb\|Professor Nerb]] steps up to say no. They learnt he [[DnD/Races/Goblin\|Goblin]] issue lasts for about 70 years.

They learnt he whereabouts of the mayor [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/NPCs/Nesha Warbrow\|Nesha Warbrow]] and make their way there. They find a sinkhole where a mine probably once was. They enter her house and speak with a [[DnD/Races/Human\|Human]] man. The group tries to charm her, [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/Party/Valentin d’Étoile\|Valentin]] fails greatly, while [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/Party/Professor Nerb\|Professor Nerb]] succeeds and she finds him cute. She proceeds to tell them, the invading goblins have shorter ears, look more red, and evil. She continues to tell them, that the invaders consist of about 60 [[DnD/Races/Goblin\|goblins]], they take food and money from the town. Two weeks later, they came back and they kept coming. The town managed to raid the [[DnD/Races/Goblin\|Goblin]] camp, but they came back again and destroyed 4 houses. Some people had to live in the library out of desperation and not enough living spaces. They can keep sending guard, but the issue never stops.

They talk about the next steps, when a commoner man bursts in asking about what [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/NPCs/Nesha Warbrow\|Nesha Warbrow]] is doing about her brother who is missing. He is very disturbed. About 6 people are missing for about a month, but they don't know why.

They go to sleep in a camp, while [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/Party/Valentin d’Étoile\|Valentin]] checks the library for a book about the [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/History/Great Racial Wars\|Great Racial Wars]] and then sleeps on the roof of the library.

In the morning the [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/Party/Party\|Party]] heads to the armory as instructed by [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/NPCs/Nesha Warbrow\|Nesha Warbrow]] and meet with [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/NPCs/Ollitur\|Ollitur]], [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/NPCs/Rakk\|Rakk]], [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/NPCs/Raleigh\|Raleigh]], and [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/NPCs/Altra\|Altra]] who tell them which direction to go in order to find the [[DnD/Races/Goblin\|Goblin]] camp.
