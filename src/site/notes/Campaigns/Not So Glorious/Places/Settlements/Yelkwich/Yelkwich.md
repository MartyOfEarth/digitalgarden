## About
[[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Places/Settlements/Yelkwich/Yelkwich\|Yelkwich]] is a bustling town situated along the rushing banks of the [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Places/Landscapes/Serpentine River\|Serpentine River]] and carved directly into the sheer cliffs of the [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Places/Landscapes/Ironvein Mountains\|Ironvein Mountains]]. It is a capital city, and considered a titan of trade and history.

## History
Founded over five hundred years ago in the bloody crucible of the [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/History/Great Racial Wars\|Great Racial Wars]], the settlement began as a desperate sanctuary discovered by a visionary [[DnD/Races/Dragonborn\|dragonborne]]. Tucked away in the shadow of the peaks, the [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Places/Landscapes/Ironvein Mountains\|mountain]] provided vast, rich veins of ore for forging weapons, while the [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Places/Landscapes/Serpentine River\|river]] offered a steady food supply and a direct gateway to the open sea. What began as a hidden fortress built for survival soon transformed into the continent’s most powerful engine of commerce.

## Now
Today, [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Places/Settlements/Yelkwich/Yelkwich\|Yelkwich]] is the beating heart of global trade. Adventurers from across the realm flock to its legendary guildhalls seeking fame, fortune, and high-paying contracts. Where heroes gather, gold follows—drawing ambitious merchants who line the streets with rare goods, exotic beasts, and enchanted wares.

The city itself is dramatically split into two distinct worlds:
