**The Leaky Tap** is a tavern located in [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Places/Settlements/Yelkwich/Inner Yelkwich/Inner Yelkwich\|Inner Yelkwich]], notable for its socioeconomically diverse clientele that ranges from the upper classes to disreputable and underworld figures. A big fight occurred there during [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Sessions/Session 1\|Session 1]] of The [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Not So Glorious\|Not So Glorious]] campaign.
