Sprawling along the base of the [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Places/Landscapes/Ironvein Mountains\|mountain]], this bustling hub is as far as most visitors ever tread. It is a vibrant, non-stop labyrinth of rowdy taverns, guildhouses, warm inns, and residential quarters. At its heart lies the Grand Market, full of dealers from all walks of life, it offers countless trinkets and goodies, if one can pay the toll.
