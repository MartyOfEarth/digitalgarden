Poor town being attacked by [[DnD/Races/Goblin\|goblins]] about three days of travel by boat from [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Places/Settlements/Yelkwich/Yelkwich\|Yelkwich]].

There is a dock with about 10 stations. When the [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/Party/Party\|Party]] arrives it is almost entirely empty. 

There are palaces, empty streets and raised mountains in the background.

There is a butcher shop.

Majority of the population is [[DnD/Races/Human\|Humans]], some [[DnD/Races/Orc\|Orcs]], [[DnD/Races/Tiefling\|Tieflings]], [[DnD/Races/Yuan-ti\|yuan-tis]].

It is kind of a dead end town with mostly low level races.

There is a part of the town, a whole wing, that's destroyed, completely empty, and blocked off.

There is a winding road leading upphill towards a nicer area of the town where the Mayor [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/NPCs/Nesha Warbrow\|Nesha Warbrow]] has a house. Close to it is a crater where the mountain has fall upon itself.
