Poor town being attacked by [[DnD/Races/Goblin\|goblins]] about three days of travel by boat from [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Places/Settlements/Yelkwich/Yelkwich\|Yelkwich]].

There is a dock with about 10 stations. When the [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/Party/Party\|Party]] arrives it is almost entirely empty. 

There are palaces, empty streets and raised mountains in the background.
