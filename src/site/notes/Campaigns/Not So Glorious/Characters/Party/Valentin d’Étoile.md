**Valentin d’Étoile** is a male [[DnD/Races/Starry Child\|Starry Child]] [[DnD/Classes/Bard\|Bard]]. Standing at 6 feet 3 inches *(191 cm)*, d'Étoile is physically notable for maintaining a higher-than-average body temperature and for his deep affinity with nature and the cosmos. 

## Personal life and characteristics

d'Étoile is fluent in [[DnD/Languages/Common\|Common]], [[DnD/Languages/Elvish\|Elvish]], and sign language. He practices strict vegetarianism and is recognized for his extensive knowledge of fine wines and viticulture. Demonstrating a strong bond with fauna, d'Étoile possesses the ability to communicate with animals, such as his beloved horse [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/Other/Cesario\|Cesario]].

Drawn to the open sky, d'Étoile habitually avoids sleeping indoors, preferring to spend nights outdoors under the stars and moon.

## Astronomy and music

d'Étoile keeps a detailed astronomical journal, routinely charting and tracing celestial constellations. He maintains a profound spiritual veneration for the moon, personified as *La Lune*, his mother. As part of a nightly ritual, he performs the song *Tant d’Étoiles* in her honor.

### Valentin’s Song
Valentin’s lullaby, [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Lore/Tant d’étoiles\|Tant d’étoiles]], translated as *So Many Stars*, is performed nightly by d'Étoile under the open sky:

**Dors, mon petit astre, la nuit descend,**  
*Sleep, my little star, the night is falling upon us,*

**Il y a tant d’étoiles qui brillent là-haut,**

*The moon listens to you, she chases away the dark.*  
**Il y a tant d’étoiles pour te protéger,**  
*There are so many stars to protect you,*  
**Dors, mon cher enfant, jusqu’au jour caché.**  
*Sleep my dear child, until the morning comes.*
