Raleigh is a [[DnD/Races/Human\|Human]] male who serves as a knight in [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Places/Settlements/Westwend\|Westwend]]. He has long blond hair. He's the archer for the knights. He is perceptive, squishy, and can find at range.
