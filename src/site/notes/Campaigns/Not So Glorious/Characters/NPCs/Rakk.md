Rakk is a eagle [[DnD/Races/Aarakocra\|Aarakocra]] who serves as a knight in [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Places/Settlements/Westwend\|Westwend]].
He's good with a sling and an eye in the sky of the knights. Not very strong, but he is quick and can use a sling and a pike. Probably the least important of the knights.
