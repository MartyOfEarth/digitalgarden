Altra is a female [[DnD/Races/Yuan-ti\|Yuan-ti]] who serves as a knight in [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Places/Settlements/Westwend\|Westwend]]. A brute force for the knights. She is moody, but very intelligent and observent. She knows about history and can make weapons.
