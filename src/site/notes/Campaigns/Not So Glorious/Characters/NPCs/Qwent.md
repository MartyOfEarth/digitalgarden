Qwent is a [[DnD/Races/Human\|Human]] male and a low-level [[DnD/Classes/Guild Master\|Guild Master]], best known as the author of [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Items/Q's Scroll\|Q's Scroll]].

### Appearance
When first encountered by the [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/Party/Party\|Party]] in his house, he wears a well-maintained leather outfit. His attire consists of a coat adorned with remnants of past treasures, a belt holding various trinkets, and large, patched boots that show roughly one to two years of wear. 

### Narrative Role
Desperate for assistance, he recruits the [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Characters/Party/Party\|Party]] to save the town of [[Campaigns/Not So Glorious/Places/Settlements/Westwend\|Westwend]] in exchange for monetary compensation. Despite his circumstances, he exhibits an enthusiastic and generally cheerful attitude.
