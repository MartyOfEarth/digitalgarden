**Script:** Unique

**Summary:** Sound travels fastest through solid objects, and as such, Terran is potentially the loudest of all the [[DnD/Languages/Languages\|languages]]. Deep, booming rumbling travelling through the very mantle of the planet can be interpreted as speech to those trained in the subtle and complex art of Terran speech. 

**Sounds:** Terran is the sound of the earth rumbling, and of stone scraping against stone.

**Appearance:** Terran has no written [[DnD/Languages/Languages\|language]], but its knowledge can be stored in the form of magnetism. A rock charged with the energy of a Terran speaker can reveal hidden messages when held by another.
