**Script:** Claw

**Summary:** Aarakocra is a language for the birds, and can be extremely difficult for a non-beaked creature to reproduce. 

**Sounds:** The [[DnD/Languages/.md\|language]] is notable in the fact that it has many ways of saying the same thing, depending on the speakers mood. The same thought can be expressed in a loud, shrill tone, or a delightful tune of birdcalls.

**Appearance:** Aarakocra is probably the most elaborate of all the Claw-script [[DnD/Languages/.md\|languages]], due to the increased dexterity of [[DnD/Races/Aarakocra\|Aarakocran]] talons over other types of claws.
