**Script:** Chisel

**Summary:** Jotun is a bizarre [[DnD/Languages/.md\|language]] - it is possible to convey deep intellectual meaning with it, but difficult to have casual conversation. As such, the written [[DnD/Languages/.md\|language]] is far more elaborate than the spoken [[DnD/Languages/.md\|language]]. 

**Sounds:** Due to the nature of giants, Jotun can only really be spoken at a shout, and in a deep, booming voice. For this reason, many humanoid [[DnD/Races/.md\|races]] struggle to speak it with any level of clarity. Prolonged conversation with a giant can cause hearing loss.

**Appearance:** The written [[DnD/Languages/.md\|language]] of the giants is a beautiful thing to behind - constructed by a series of elaborate rune-stamps that giants can pound into stone with great strength and precision. There is a huge variety of runes, and owning a complete set of all the characters is a significant feat in itself.
