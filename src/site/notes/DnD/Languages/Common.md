# Common
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Standard
> - Script:: Pen

**Summary:** Common is, as the name suggests, the most commonly spoken [[DnD/Languages/Languages\|Languages]] in the world. It was a [[DnD/Languages/Languages\|Languages]] formed by the fusion of many cultures all slowly combining together over centuries.

**Sounds:** Common is heavily impacted by the other [[DnD/Languages/Languages\|Languages]] that the speaker knows, taking on elements of those [[DnD/Languages/Languages\|Languages]]. For example, a speaker who also knows [[DnD/Languages/Elvish\|elvish]] will speak in a more sing-song fashion, while a speaker who also knows [[DnD/Languages/Gnomish\|gnomish]] will speak so quickly that it can still resemble the [[DnD/Languages/Gnomish\|gnomish]] [[DnD/Languages/Languages\|Languages]], and be difficult to understand.

**Appearance:** Common writing is unique in that it was the only script that had some amount of conscious design behind it, as opposed to developing naturally over centuries, as with other [[DnD/Languages/Languages\|Languages]]. Its characters are simple, and constructed entirely of straight lines and right-angles, making it easy to write in, no matter what tool is being used.
