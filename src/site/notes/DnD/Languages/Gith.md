# Gith
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Exotic
> - Script:: Claw

**Summary:** The Gith [[DnD/Languages/Languages\|Languages]] has two distinct dialects; one for the [[DnD/Races/Githyanki\|Githyanki]], and one for the [[DnD/Races/Githzerai\|Githzerai]]. Being familiar with one of them means a person can generally decipher the other. 

**Sounds:** The spoken [[DnD/Languages/Languages\|Languages]] of the Gith is a bizarre, rough one, and comes across as a far more organised, understandable version of [[DnD/Languages/Deep Speech\|Deep Speech]]. It contains none of the unspeakable noises of [[DnD/Languages/Deep Speech\|Deep Speech]], but learning how to pronounce all the strange, screeching sounds correctly requires some intense vocal training. 

**Appearance:** The written word if the Gith is quite unique in appearance. Each sentence is written in a circular shape, resulting in a page full of circles that can be a little difficult to read for an untrained person.
