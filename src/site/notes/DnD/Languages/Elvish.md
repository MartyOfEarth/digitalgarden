# Elvish
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Standard
> - Script:: Brush

**Summary:** Elvish is a complex [[DnD/Languages/Languages\|Languages]], with a great deal of subtlety and intricate grammar. It is very easy to say the wrong thing just by putting emphasis on the wrong syllable in a word. It is a [[DnD/Languages/Common\|common language]] among bards.

**Sounds:** Elvish sounds very lyrical, and due to the importance of how each syllable is emphasised, it often sounds like people are singing when they speak. 

**Appearance:** Elvish characters are traditionally written with a paintbrush-like tool, making the characters elaborate, rounded, and loopy.
