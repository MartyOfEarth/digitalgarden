# Goblin
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Standard
> - Script:: Claw

**Summary:** Goblin is the [[DnD/Languages/Languages\|Languages]] spoken by all Goblinoids ([[DnD/Races/Goblin\|Goblins]], [[DnD/Races/Hobgoblin\|hobgoblins]], [[DnD/Races/Bugbear\|bugbear]], etc.). 

**Sounds:** [[DnD/Races/Goblin\|DnD/Races/Goblin]] is a very throat-heavy [[DnD/Languages/Languages\|Languages]], with a great deal of hissing noises. It can be very difficult to understand, especially as it is generally spoken at very low volumes, and is easily covered up by other noises in the surrounding area.

**Appearance:** [[DnD/Races/Goblin\|Goblin]] script is probably the simplest of all the written [[DnD/Languages/Languages\|Languages]], constructed of very simple scratches. It is hard to express any real depth of meaning in the written word, and most goblins will opt to write in [[DnD/Languages/Common\|common]] instead.
