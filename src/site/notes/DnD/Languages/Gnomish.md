**Script:** Chisel

**Summary:** Gnomish [[DnD/Languages/index\|language]] developed alongside [[DnD/Languages/Dwarvish\|dwarvish]], though segued into its own complex [[DnD/Languages/index\|language]] as gnomes became more technically minded.

**Sounds:** Gnomish is a less guttural, more chittery [[DnD/Languages/index\|language]], and is spoken a great deal faster than [[DnD/Languages/Dwarvish\|dwarvish]] and most other [[DnD/Languages/index\|languages]].

**Appearance:** Gnomish writing is designed to convey a great deal of information in a short space. The [[DnD/Languages/index\|language]] contains thousands of characters that interlink with each other, making a gnomish text look almost like a blueprint.
