# Orcish
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Standard
> - Script:: Brush

**Summary:** Orcish is as much a visual [[DnD/Languages/Languages\|Languages]] as it is a audible one. The bodily gestures of the speaker are important to understanding the context and meaning of their words.

**Sounds:** Orcish is a loud, forceful [[DnD/Languages/Languages\|Languages]], with many of its speakers insisting that it only sounds correct if you are yelling it at the top of your lungs. 

**Appearance:** Orcish is written with a brush, and many of its characters resemble the shapes of veins.
