## Yuan-ti
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Humanoid (Serpentine)
> - Size:: Medium or Small
> - Speed:: 30 ft.

- **Overview:** Serpent-folk born of ancient serpentine rites, possessing cold pragmatism, scaly features, and innate snake magic.
- **Characteristics & Traits:**
  - **Darkvision:** 60 ft.
  - **Magic Resistance:** Advantage on all saving throws against spells.
  - **Poison Resilience:** Advantage on saving throws against poison, and resistance to poison damage.
  - **Serpentine Spellcasting:** Innately knows the *Poison Spray* cantrip. Can cast *Animal Friendship* (snakes only) at will, and *Suggestion* (at 3rd level) once per long rest.
