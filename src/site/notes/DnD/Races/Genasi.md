## Genasi (Air)
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Humanoid
> - Size:: Medium or Small
> - Speed:: 35 ft.

- **Overview:** Mortals carrying the elemental heritage of djinn and the Endless Sky, marked by breeze-blown hair and light steps.
- **Characteristics & Traits:**
  - **Darkvision:** 60 ft.
  - **Unending Breath:** Can hold breath indefinitely while not incapacitated.
  - **Lightning Resistance:** Resistance to lightning damage.
  - **Mingle with the Wind:** Can cast *Shocking Grasp*, *Feather Fall* (at 3rd level), and *Levitate* (at 5th level) without material components.

## Genasi (Earth)
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Humanoid
> - Size:: Medium or Small
> - Speed:: 30 ft.

- **Overview:** Mortals with bloodlines tracing back to dao and the Elemental Plane of Earth, featuring rocky skin or gemstone eyes.
- **Characteristics & Traits:**
  - **Darkvision:** 60 ft.
  - **Earth Walk:** Can move across difficult terrain made of earth or stone without extra movement cost.

## Genasi (Water)
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Humanoid
> - Size:: Medium or Small
> - Speed:: 30 ft., Swim 30 ft.

- **Overview:** Mortals with ancestral ties to marids and the Plane of Water, possessing blue- or green-hued skin and amphibious traits.
- **Characteristics & Traits:**
  - **Amphibious:** Able to breathe air and water.
  - **Acid Resistance:** Resistance to acid damage.
  - **Darkvision:** 60 ft.
  - **Call to the Wave:** Can cast *Acid Splash*, *Create or Destroy Water* (at 3rd level), and *Water Walk* (at 5th level).

</div></div>
