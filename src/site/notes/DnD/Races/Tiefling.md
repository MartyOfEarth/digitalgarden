## Tiefling
- **Type:** Humanoid | **Size:** Medium or Small | **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **Overview:** Mortals carrying fiendish bloodlines tracing to the Lower Planes, recognized by horns, tails, and dark magic.
- **Characteristics & Traits:**
  - **Darkvision:** 60 ft.
  - **Fiendish Legacy:** Traces lineage to Abyssal, Chthonic, or Infernal origins, granting relevant damage resistance (Poison, Necrotic, or Fire) and progressive magical spells.
  - **Otherworldly Presence:** Innately knows the *Thaumaturgy* cantrip.
