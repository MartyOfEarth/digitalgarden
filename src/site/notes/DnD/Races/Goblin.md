## Goblin
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Humanoid (Goblinoid)
> - Size:: Small
> - Speed:: 30 ft.

- **Overview:** Tenacious, cunning survivors accustomed to being underdogs, turning agility and fury into lethal weapons.
- **Characteristics & Traits:**
  - **Darkvision:** 60 ft.
  - **Fey Ancestry:** Advantage on saving throws against being Charmed.
  - **Fury of the Small:** When damaging a creature larger than themselves, can deal extra damage equal to their proficiency bonus (usable proficiency bonus times per long rest).
  - **Nimble Escape:** Able to take the Disengage or Hide action as a bonus action on each of their turns.
