## Dwarf
- **Type:** Humanoid | **Size:** Medium | **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **Overview:** Stout, enduring folk renowned for subterranean architecture, master smithing, and resilient constitutions.
- **Characteristics & Traits:**
  - **Darkvision:** 120 ft.
  - **Dwarven Resilience:** Advantage on saving throws against poison, and resistance to poison damage.
  - **Dwarven Toughness:** Hit point maximum increases by 1 per level.
  - **Stonecunning:** Can gain Tremorsense out to 60 ft. on stone surfaces for 10 minutes as a bonus action (usable proficiency bonus times per long rest).
