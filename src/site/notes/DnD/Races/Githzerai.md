## Githzerai
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Humanoid
> - Size:: Medium
> - Speed:: 30 ft.

- **Overview:** Monastic, introspective wanderers of Limbo who use disciplined mental fortitude to bend chaotic reality.
- **Characteristics & Traits:**
  - **Mental Discipline:** Advantage on saving throws to avoid or end the Charmed and Frightened conditions.
  - **Githzerai Psionics:** Can cast *Mage Hand* (invisible), *Shield* (at 3rd level), and *Detect Thoughts* (at 5th level).
  - **Psychic Resistance:** Resistance to psychic damage.
