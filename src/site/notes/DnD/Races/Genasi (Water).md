## Genasi (Water)
- **Type:** Humanoid | **Size:** Medium or Small | **Speed:** 30 ft., Swim 30 ft.
- **Overview:** Mortals with ancestral ties to marids and the Plane of Water, possessing blue- or green-hued skin and amphibious traits.
- **Characteristics & Traits:**
  - **Amphibious:** Able to breathe air and water.
  - **Acid Resistance:** Resistance to acid damage.
  - **Darkvision:** 60 ft.
  - **Call to the Wave:** Can cast *Acid Splash*, *Create or Destroy Water* (at 3rd level), and *Water Walk* (at 5th level).
