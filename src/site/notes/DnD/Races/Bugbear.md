## Bugbear
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Humanoid (Goblinoid)
> - Size:: Medium
> - Speed:: 30 ft.

- **Overview:** Large, furry goblinoids with deceptively quiet movement and disproportionately long limbs, born with predatory instincts.
- **Characteristics & Traits:**
  - **Darkvision:** 60 ft.
  - **Fey Ancestry:** Advantage on saving throws against being charmed.
  - **Long-Limbed:** Melee attack reach is 5 ft. greater than normal during their turn.
  - **Powerful Build:** Counts as Large for carrying and lifting capacity.
  - **Sneaky:** Proficient in Stealth; able to move through spaces small enough for Small creatures without squeezing.
  - **Surprise Attack:** Deals an extra 2d6 damage to a creature hit before it has taken a turn in combat.
