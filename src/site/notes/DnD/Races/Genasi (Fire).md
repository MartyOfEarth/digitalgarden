## Genasi (Fire)
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Humanoid
> - Size:: Medium or Small
> - Speed:: 30 ft.

- **Overview:** Mortals touched by efreet and the Plane of Fire, displaying smoldering hair, warm skin, or ember-toned eyes.
- **Characteristics & Traits:**
  - **Darkvision:** 60 ft.
  - **Fire Resistance:** Resistance to fire damage.
  - **Reach to the Blaze:** Can cast *Produce Flame*, *Burning Hands* (at 3rd level), and *Flame Blade* (at 5th level).
