## Tortle
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Humanoid (Reptilian)
> - Size:: Medium or Small
> - Speed:: 30 ft.

- **Overview:** Wandering turtle-folk who carry their shelters on their backs, exhibiting calm minds and simple lifestyles.
- **Characteristics & Traits:**
  - **Claws:** Unarmed strike deals 1d6 + Strength modifier slashing damage.
  - **Hold Breath:** Can hold breath for up to 1 hour at a time.
  - **Natural Armor:** Base AC is 17 (cannot wear armor, but benefits from shields).
  - **Shell Defense:** Can withdraw into their shell as an action for +4 AC and advantage on Strength/Constitution saves (reduces speed to 0 and gives disadvantage on Dexterity saves).
