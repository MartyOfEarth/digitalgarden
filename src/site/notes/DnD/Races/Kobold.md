## Kobold
- **Type:** Humanoid (Reptilian) | **Size:** Small | **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **Overview:** Small reptilian humanoids claiming dragon blood, known for clever teamwork, underground mining, and trap building.
- **Characteristics & Traits:**
  - **Darkvision:** 60 ft.
  - **[[DnD/Languages/Draconic\|Draconic]] Cry:** As a bonus action, yells to grant themselves and allies advantage on attack rolls against nearby enemies within 10 ft. until their next turn ends.
  - **Kobold Legacy:** Possesses one distinct lineage trait: an arcane cantrip, skill in craftsmanship, or defiance against fear.
