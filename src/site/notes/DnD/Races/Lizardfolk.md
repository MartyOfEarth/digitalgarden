## Lizardfolk
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Humanoid (Reptilian)
> - Size:: Medium
> - Speed:: 30 ft., Swim 30 ft.

- **Overview:** Pragmatic, cold-blooded marsh dwellers who craft gear from nature's bounty and rarely waste resources.
- **Characteristics & Traits:**
  - **Bite:** Unarmed strike deals 1d6 + Strength modifier piercing damage.
  - **Hungry Jaws:** Bonus action bite attack; on a hit, gains temporary hit points equal to proficiency bonus (usable proficiency bonus times per long rest).
  - **Natural Armor:** Base AC is 13 + Dexterity modifier when unarmored.
  - **Nature's Intuition:** Proficient in two skills chosen from Animal Handling, Medicine, Nature, Perception, Stealth, or Survival.
