# Giant Lizard
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Beast
> - Size:: Large
> - CR:: 1/4
> - AC:: 12
> - HP:: 19 (3d10 + 3)
> - Speed:: 30 ft.
> - Capacity:: 450 lbs
> - Tags:: #monster #mount #underdark #reptile

A subterranean and swamp-dwelling reptile favored by drow, [[DnD/Bestiary/Kobold\|kobolds]], and [[DnD/Races/Lizardfolk\|lizardfolk]]. Variants often possess adhesive toe pads allowing them to scale walls and ceilings effortlessly.

- **Str:** 15 (+2) | **Dex:** 12 (+1) | **Con:** 13 (+1) | **Int:** 2 (-4) | **Wis:** 10 (+0) | **Cha:** 5 (-3)
- **Traits:**
  - **Spider Climb (Variant):** The lizard can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.
- **Actions:**
  - **Bite:** Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 6 (1d8 + 2) piercing damage.
