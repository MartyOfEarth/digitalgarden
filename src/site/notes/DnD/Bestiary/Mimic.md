# Mimic
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Monstrosity
> - Size:: Medium
> - CR:: 2
> - AC:: 12 (natural)
> - HP:: 58 (9d8 + 18)
> - Speed:: 15 ft.
> - Tags:: #monster #dungeon-staple #monstrosity #dungeon

Predatory shapeshifters capable of altering skin texture to perfectly disguise themselves as inanimate wooden or stone items (chests, doors, barrels).

- **Str:** 17 (+3) | **Dex:** 12 (+1) | **Con:** 15 (+2) | **Int:** 5 (-3) | **Wis:** 13 (+1) | **Cha:** 8 (-1)
- **Skills:** Stealth +5
- **Condition Immunities:** Prone
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft.
- **Traits:**
  - **Shapechanger:** Can use its action to polymorph into an object or back into its amorphous form.
  - **Adhesive:** In object form, adheres to anything that touches it. An adhered creature is Grappled (escape DC 13).
  - **False Appearance:** While motionless in object form, it is indistinguishable from an ordinary object.
- **Actions:**
  - **Pseudopod:** Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 7 (1d8 + 3) bludgeoning damage.
  - **Bite:** Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 7 (1d8 + 3) piercing damage plus 4 (1d8) acid damage.
