# Giant Spider
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Beast
> - Size:: Large
> - CR:: 1
> - AC:: 14 (natural)
> - HP:: 26 (4d10 + 4)
> - Speed:: 30 ft., Climb 30 ft.
> - Tags:: #monster #summon #wildshape #underdark #beast

A predatory horror native to dark caverns and subterranean depths. Frequently summoned or shifted into for its climbing agility, poison bite, and ranged web restraints.

- **Str:** 14 (+2) | **Dex:** 16 (+3) | **Con:** 12 (+1) | **Int:** 2 (-4) | **Wis:** 11 (+0) | **Cha:** 4 (-3)
- **Skills:** Stealth +7
- **Senses:** Blindsight 10 ft., Darkvision 60 ft.
- **Traits:**
  - **Spider Climb:** Can climb difficult surfaces, including ceilings, without an ability check.
  - **Web Walker:** Ignores movement restrictions caused by webbing.
- **Actions:**
  - **Bite:** Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. Hit: 7 (1d8 + 3) piercing damage plus 9 (2d8) poison damage (DC 11 Con save for half; target reduced to 0 HP is stable but poisoned/paralyzed for 1 hour).
  - **Web (Recharge 5–6):** Ranged Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, range 30/60 ft., one creature. Hit: Target is Restrained by webbing (escape DC 12).
