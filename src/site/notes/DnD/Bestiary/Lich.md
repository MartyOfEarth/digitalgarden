# Lich
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Undead
> - Size:: Medium
> - CR:: 21
> - AC:: 17 (natural)
> - HP:: 135 (18d8 + 54)
> - Speed:: 30 ft.
> - Tags:: #monster #boss #legendary #undead #spellcaster

Supreme undead [[DnD/Classes/Wizard\|wizards]] who surrendered their mortal souls to achieve eternal life, binding their vitality to a hidden phylactery.

- **Str:** 11 (+0) | **Dex:** 16 (+3) | **Con:** 16 (+3) | **Int:** 20 (+5) | **Wis:** 14 (+2) | **Cha:** 16 (+3)
- **Damage Immunities:** Poison; nonmagical physical attacks
- **Condition Immunities:** Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Poisoned
- **Senses:** Truesight 120 ft.
- **Traits:**
  - **Legendary Resistance (3/Day):** If the lich fails a save, it can choose to succeed instead.
  - **Rejuvenation:** Regains a new body in 1d10 days unless its phylactery is found and destroyed.
  - **Spellcasting:** 18th-level [[DnD/Classes/Wizard\|wizard]] caster (DC 20, +12 to hit). Prepares *Power Word Kill*, *Disintegrate*, *Finger of Death*, *Globe of Invulnerability*, *Shield*, *Counterspell*.
- **Actions:**
  - **Paralyzing Touch:** Melee Spell Attack: +12 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. Hit: 10 (3d6) cold damage; DC 18 Con save or paralyzed for 1 minute.
- **Legendary Actions (3/Round):** Cantrip, Paralyzing Touch (costs 2), Frightening Gaze (costs 2), Disrupt Life (costs 3, DC 18 Con save or 21 necrotic damage).
