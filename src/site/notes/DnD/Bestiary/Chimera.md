# Chimera
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Monstrosity
> - Size:: Large
> - CR:: 6
> - AC:: 14 (natural)
> - HP:: 114 (12d10 + 48)
> - Speed:: 30 ft., Fly 60 ft.
> - Tags:: #monster #monstrosity #mountain

A savage hybrid beast possessing the heads of a goat, a lion, and a red dragon, combining brute force with a fiery breath weapon.

- **Str:** 19 (+4) | **Dex:** 11 (+0) | **Con:** 19 (+4) | **Int:** 3 (-4) | **Wis:** 14 (+2) | **Cha:** 10 (+0)
- **Skills:** Perception +8
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft.
- **Actions:**
  - **Multiattack:** Makes three attacks: one with its bite, one with its horns, and one with its claws. When fire breath is available, can replace bite with it.
  - **Bite:** Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 11 (2d6 + 4) piercing damage.
  - **Horns:** Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 10 (1d12 + 4) bludgeoning damage.
  - **Claws:** Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 11 (2d6 + 4) slashing damage.
  - **Fire Breath (Recharge 5–6):** 15-foot cone; DC 15 Dex save or take 31 (7d8) fire damage (half on success).
