# Mind Flayer
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Aberration
> - Size:: Medium
> - CR:: 7
> - AC:: 15 (breastplate)
> - HP:: 71 (13d8 + 13)
> - Speed:: 30 ft.
> - Tags:: #monster #boss #aberration #underdark #psionic

Psionic subterranean enslavers (illithids) who harvest humanoid brains for nourishment and mental thralls for their subterranean hive cities.

- **Str:** 11 (+0) | **Dex:** 12 (+1) | **Con:** 12 (+1) | **Int:** 19 (+4) | **Wis:** 17 (+3) | **Cha:** 17 (+3)
- **Senses:** Darkvision 120 ft.
- **Traits:**
  - **Magic Resistance:** Advantage on saves against spells and magical effects.
  - **Innate Spellcasting (Psionics):** At will: *Detect Thoughts*, *Levitate*. 1/day each: *Dominate Monster*, *Plane Shift* (self only).
- **Actions:**
  - **Tentacles:** Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. Hit: 15 (2d10 + 4) psychic damage, and target is Grappled (escape DC 15) and Stunned.
  - **Extract Brain:** Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one incapacitated humanoid grappled by the mind flayer. Hit: 55 (10d10) piercing damage. If this reduces the target to 0 HP, kills the target by extracting its brain.
  - **Mind Blast (Recharge 5–6):** 60-ft. cone; DC 15 Int save or take 22 (4d8 + 4) psychic damage and be Stunned for 1 minute.
