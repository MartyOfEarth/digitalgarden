# Couatl
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Celestial
> - Size:: Medium
> - CR:: 4
> - AC:: 19 (natural)
> - HP:: 97 (13d8 + 39)
> - Speed:: 30 ft., Fly 90 ft.
> - Tags:: #monster #celestial #planar

A benevolent, iridescent serpent with feathered rainbow wings, created by good deities to guard ancient sanctums and sacred prophecies.

- **Str:** 16 (+3) | **Dex:** 20 (+5) | **Con:** 17 (+3) | **Int:** 18 (+4) | **Wis:** 20 (+5) | **Cha:** 18 (+4)
- **Damage Resistances:** Radiant; nonmagical physical damage
- **Damage Immunities:** Psychic
- **Traits:**
  - **Magic Resistance:** Advantage on saves against spells and magical effects.
  - **Shielded Mind:** Immune to scrying and mind-reading.
  - **Innate Spellcasting:** At will: *Detect Evil and Good*, *Sanctuary*. 3/day each: *Bless*, *Create Food and Water*, *Cure Wounds*, *Lesser Restoration*. 1/day each: *Dream*, *Greater Restoration*, *Scrying*.
- **Actions:**
  - **Bite:** Melee Weapon Attack: +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 8 (1d6 + 5) piercing damage, and DC 13 Con save or poisoned and unconscious for 24 hours.
  - **Constrict:** Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 10 ft., one creature. Hit: 10 (2d6 + 3) bludgeoning damage, and target is Grappled (escape DC 15) and Restrained.
  - **Change Shape:** Magically polymorphs into a humanoid or beast that has a CR no higher than its own.
