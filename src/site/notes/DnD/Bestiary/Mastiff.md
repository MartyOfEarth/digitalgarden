Massive, loyal hounds frequently kept as perimeter guardians, hunting beasts, and dependable riding mounts for Small humanoids.

- **Str:** 13 (+1) | **Dex:** 14 (+2) | **Con:** 12 (+1) | **Int:** 3 (-4) | **Wis:** 12 (+1) | **Cha:** 7 (-2)
- **Traits:**
  - **Keen Hearing and Smell:** Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing or smell.
- **Actions:**
  - **Bite:** Melee Weapon Attack: +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 4 (1d6 + 1) piercing damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 11 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.
