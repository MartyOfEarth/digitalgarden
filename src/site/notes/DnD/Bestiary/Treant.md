# Treant
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Plant
> - Size:: Huge
> - CR:: 9
> - AC:: 16 (natural)
> - HP:: 138 (12d12 + 60)
> - Speed:: 30 ft.
> - Tags:: #monster #boss #plant #forest

Sentient, ancient tree-shepherds that awaken during times of ecological crisis to defend sacred groves, crushing loggers and stone structures alike.

- **Str:** 23 (+6) | **Dex:** 8 (-1) | **Con:** 21 (+5) | **Int:** 12 (+1) | **Wis:** 16 (+3) | **Cha:** 12 (+1)
- **Damage Vulnerabilities:** Fire
- **Damage Resistances:** Bludgeoning, Piercing
- **Traits:**
  - **False Appearance:** Indistinguishable from a normal tree while motionless.
  - **Siege Monster:** Deals double damage to objects and structures.
- **Actions:**
  - **Multiattack:** Makes two slam attacks.
  - **Slam:** Melee Weapon Attack: +10 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 16 (3d6 + 6) bludgeoning damage.
  - **Rock:** Ranged Weapon Attack: +10 to hit, range 60/180 ft., one target. Hit: 28 (4d10 + 6) bludgeoning damage.
  - **Animate Trees (1/Day):** Magically animates one or two trees within 60 ft. for 1 day, gaining the treant's statistics.
