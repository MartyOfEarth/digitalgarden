# Ghoul
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Undead
> - Size:: Medium
> - CR:: 1
> - AC:: 12
> - HP:: 22 (5d8)
> - Speed:: 30 ft.
> - Tags:: #monster #minion #undead #dungeon

Cannibalistic undead fiends that haunt graveyards and crypts, craving rotting flesh and paralyzing the living with diseased claws.

- **Str:** 13 (+1) | **Dex:** 15 (+2) | **Con:** 10 (+0) | **Int:** 7 (-2) | **Wis:** 10 (+0) | **Cha:** 6 (-2)
- **Damage Immunities:** Poison
- **Condition Immunities:** Charmed, Exhaustion, Poisoned
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft.
- **Actions:**
  - **Bite:** Melee Weapon Attack: +2 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. Hit: 9 (2d6 + 2) piercing damage.
  - **Claws:** Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 7 (2d4 + 2) slashing damage. If target is a non-[[DnD/Races/Elf\|elf]] creature, must make a DC 10 Con save or be Paralyzed for 1 minute.
