# Displacer Beast
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Monstrosity
> - Size:: Large
> - CR:: 3
> - AC:: 13 (natural)
> - HP:: 85 (10d10 + 30)
> - Speed:: 40 ft.
> - Tags:: #monster #dungeon-staple #monstrosity #fey

A predatory feline monstrosity from the Feywild possessing six legs and two tentacle appendages, projecting an illusion that shifts its image a few feet away.

- **Str:** 18 (+4) | **Dex:** 15 (+2) | **Con:** 16 (+3) | **Int:** 6 (-2) | **Wis:** 12 (+1) | **Cha:** 8 (-1)
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft.
- **Traits:**
  - **Avoidance:** If subjected to an effect that allows a Dex save for half damage, takes no damage on success and half on failure.
  - **Displacement:** Attack rolls against the beast have disadvantage. The trait is disrupted until the end of its next turn if it takes damage.
- **Actions:**
  - **Multiattack:** Makes two tentacle attacks.
  - **Tentacle:** Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 7 (1d6 + 4) bludgeoning damage plus 3 (1d6) piercing damage.
