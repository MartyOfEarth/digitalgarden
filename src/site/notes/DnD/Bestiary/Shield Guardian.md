# Shield Guardian
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Construct
> - Size:: Large
> - CR:: 7
> - AC:: 17 (natural)
> - HP:: 142 (15d10 + 60)
> - Speed:: 30 ft.
> - Tags:: #monster #construct #dungeon

A hulking animated automaton bound to an arcane control amulet, shielding its master from harm and storing a defensive or offensive spell.

- **Str:** 18 (+4) | **Dex:** 8 (-1) | **Con:** 18 (+4) | **Int:** 7 (-2) | **Wis:** 10 (+0) | **Cha:** 3 (-4)
- **Damage Immunities:** Poison
- **Condition Immunities:** Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Poisoned
- **Senses:** Blindsight 10 ft., Darkvision 60 ft.
- **Traits:**
  - **Bound:** Bound to an amulet. The guardian knows the wearer's exact distance and direction.
  - **Regeneration:** Regains 10 HP at the start of its turn if it has at least 1 HP.
  - **Spell Storing:** A spellcaster carrying the amulet can store one spell of 4th level or lower in the guardian for it to cast later.
- **Actions:**
  - **Multiattack:** Makes two fist attacks.
  - **Fist:** Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 11 (2d6 + 4) bludgeoning damage.
- **Reactions:**
  - **Shield:** Grants a +5 bonus to its master's AC against one attack that would hit, provided it is within 5 ft.
