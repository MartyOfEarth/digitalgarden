# Hill Giant
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Giant
> - Size:: Huge
> - CR:: 5
> - AC:: 13 (natural)
> - HP:: 105 (10d12 + 40)
> - Speed:: 40 ft.
> - Tags:: #monster #giant #wilderness

Dim-witted, gluttonous giants standing at the bottom of the Ordning, consuming livestock, trees, and caravans to satisfy endless hunger.

- **Str:** 21 (+5) | **Dex:** 8 (-1) | **Con:** 19 (+4) | **Int:** 5 (-3) | **Wis:** 9 (-1) | **Cha:** 6 (-2)
- **Skills:** Perception +2
- **Actions:**
  - **Multiattack:** Makes two greatclub attacks.
  - **Greatclub:** Melee Weapon Attack: +8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 18 (3d8 + 5) bludgeoning damage.
  - **Rock:** Ranged Weapon Attack: +8 to hit, range 60/240 ft., one target. Hit: 21 (3d10 + 5) bludgeoning damage.
