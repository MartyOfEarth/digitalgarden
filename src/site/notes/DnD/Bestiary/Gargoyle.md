# Gargoyle
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Elemental
> - Size:: Medium
> - CR:: 2
> - AC:: 15 (natural)
> - HP:: 52 (7d8 + 21)
> - Speed:: 30 ft., Fly 60 ft.
> - Tags:: #monster #dungeon-staple #elemental #dungeon

Cruel, winged elemental sentinels made of living stone that perch motionless upon cathedral roofs and ancient crypts to ambush interlopers.

- **Str:** 15 (+2) | **Dex:** 11 (+0) | **Con:** 16 (+3) | **Int:** 6 (-2) | **Wis:** 11 (+0) | **Cha:** 7 (-2)
- **Damage Resistances:** Bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks not made with adamantine
- **Damage Immunities:** Poison
- **Condition Immunities:** Exhaustion, Petrified, Poisoned
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft.
- **Traits:**
  - **False Appearance:** While remaining motionless, the gargoyle is indistinguishable from an inanimate stone statue.
- **Actions:**
  - **Multiattack:** Makes two attacks: one with its bite and one with its claws.
  - **Bite:** Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage.
  - **Claws:** Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 5 (1d6 + 2) slashing damage.
