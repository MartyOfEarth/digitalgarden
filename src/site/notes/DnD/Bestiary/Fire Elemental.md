# Fire Elemental
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Elemental
> - Size:: Large
> - CR:: 5
> - AC:: 13
> - HP:: 102 (12d10 + 36)
> - Speed:: 50 ft.
> - Tags:: #monster #summon #planar #elemental

A raging vortex of living flame summoned from the Elemental Plane of Fire via *Conjure Elemental* or 10th-level Moon [[DnD/Classes/Druid\|Druid]] *Elemental Wild Shape*.

- **Str:** 10 (+0) | **Dex:** 17 (+3) | **Con:** 16 (+3) | **Int:** 6 (-2) | **Wis:** 10 (+0) | **Cha:** 7 (-2)
- **Damage Resistances:** Bludgeoning, piercing, slashing from nonmagical attacks
- **Damage Immunities:** Fire, Poison
- **Condition Immunities:** Exhaustion, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained, Unconscious
- **Traits:**
  - **Fire Form:** Can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch. Can enter a hostile creature's space and stop there. Touching or hitting it with a melee attack deals 5 (1d10) fire damage.
  - **Illumination:** Sheds bright light in a 30-foot radius.
- **Actions:**
  - **Multiattack:** Makes two touch attacks.
  - **Touch:** Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 10 (2d6 + 3) fire damage. If the target is a creature, it catches fire and takes 5 (1d10) fire damage at the start of each of its turns until someone extinguishes it.
