# Wight
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Undead
> - Size:: Medium
> - CR:: 3
> - AC:: 14 (studded leather)
> - HP:: 45 (6d8 + 18)
> - Speed:: 30 ft.
> - Tags:: #monster #minion #undead #dungeon

Malevolent, intelligent undead warriors driven by eternal hatred of all life, reanimating slain humanoids as obedient zombie thralls.

- **Str:** 16 (+3) | **Dex:** 14 (+2) | **Con:** 16 (+3) | **Int:** 10 (+0) | **Wis:** 13 (+1) | **Cha:** 15 (+2)
- **Skills:** Perception +3, Stealth +4
- **Damage Resistances:** Necrotic; nonmagical physical damage not made with silvered weapons
- **Damage Immunities:** Poison
- **Condition Immunities:** Exhaustion, Poisoned
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft.
- **Traits:**
  - **Sunlight Sensitivity:** Disadvantage on attack rolls and sight-based Perception checks in direct sunlight.
- **Actions:**
  - **Multiattack:** Makes two longsword attacks or two longbow attacks. Can replace one attack with Life Drain.
  - **Life Drain:** Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. Hit: 5 (1d6 + 2) necrotic damage; DC 13 Con save or max HP reduced by damage taken. Slain humanoids rise as zombies under the wight's control 24 hours later.
  - **Longsword:** Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 6 (1d8 + 2) slashing damage.
  - **Longbow:** Ranged Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, range 150/600 ft., one target. Hit: 6 (1d8 + 2) piercing damage.
