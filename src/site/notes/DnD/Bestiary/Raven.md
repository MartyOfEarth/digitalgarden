# Raven
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Beast
> - Size:: Tiny
> - CR:: 0
> - AC:: 12
> - HP:: 1 (1d4 - 1)
> - Speed:: 10 ft., Fly 50 ft.
> - Tags:: #monster #familiar #aerial #beast

Intelligent corvids associated with mystery and omens. Ravens can mimic simple phrases and sounds, making them effective messengers and alarm systems.

- **Str:** 2 (-4) | **Dex:** 14 (+2) | **Con:** 8 (-1) | **Int:** 2 (-4) | **Wis:** 12 (+1) | **Cha:** 6 (-2)
- **Skills:** Perception +3
- **Traits:**
  - **Mimicry:** The raven can mimic simple sounds it has heard. A creature that hears the sounds can tell they are imitations with a successful DC 10 Wisdom (Insight) check.
- **Actions:**
  - **Beak:** Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 1 piercing damage.
