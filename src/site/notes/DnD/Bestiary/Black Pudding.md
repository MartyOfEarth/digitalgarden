# Black Pudding
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Ooze
> - Size:: Large
> - CR:: 4
> - AC:: 7
> - HP:: 85 (10d10 + 30)
> - Speed:: 20 ft., Climb 20 ft.
> - Tags:: #monster #dungeon-staple #ooze #underdark

A corrosive, pitch-black mass of acid that strips metal and wood down to nothing, flowing silently along dungeon walls and crevices.

- **Str:** 16 (+3) | **Dex:** 5 (-3) | **Con:** 16 (+3) | **Int:** 1 (-5) | **Wis:** 6 (-2) | **Cha:** 1 (-5)
- **Damage Immunities:** Acid, Cold, Lightning, Slashing
- **Condition Immunities:** Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Exhaustion, Frightened, Prone
- **Senses:** Blindsight 60 ft.
- **Traits:**
  - **Amorphous:** Move through spaces as narrow as 1 inch wide without squeezing.
  - **Corrosive Form:** Touching or hitting it with a nonmagical metal weapon corrodes the weapon (-1 penalty; destroyed at -5).
  - **Spider Climb:** Climb difficult surfaces and ceilings without checks.
- **Actions:**
  - **Pseudopod:** Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 6 (1d6 + 3) bludgeoning plus 18 (4d8) acid damage. Nonmagical armor takes permanent -1 penalty to AC.
- **Reactions:**
  - **Split:** If a Large or Medium pudding takes lightning or slashing damage, it splits into two new jellies (if at least 10 HP).
