# Manticore
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Monstrosity
> - Size:: Large
> - CR:: 3
> - AC:: 14 (natural)
> - HP:: 68 (8d10 + 24)
> - Speed:: 30 ft., Fly 50 ft.
> - Tags:: #monster #monstrosity #flying #mountain

A vicious predator combining a humanoid face, a lion's body, leathery bat wings, and a tail covered in iron-hard spikes it fires from distance.

- **Str:** 17 (+3) | **Dex:** 16 (+3) | **Con:** 17 (+3) | **Int:** 7 (-2) | **Wis:** 12 (+1) | **Cha:** 8 (-1)
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft.
- **Actions:**
  - **Multiattack:** Makes three attacks: one bite and two claws, or three tail spikes.
  - **Bite:** Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 7 (1d8 + 3) piercing damage.
  - **Claw:** Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 6 (1d6 + 3) slashing damage.
  - **Tail Spike:** Ranged Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, range 100/200 ft., one target. Hit: 7 (1d8 + 3) piercing damage.
