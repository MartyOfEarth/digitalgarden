# Owlbear
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Monstrosity
> - Size:: Large
> - CR:: 3
> - AC:: 13 (natural)
> - HP:: 59 (7d10 + 21)
> - Speed:: 40 ft.
> - Tags:: #monster #dungeon-staple #monstrosity #forest

A cross between a giant bear and an owl, renowned for ferocious temperament, razor-sharp talons, and a bone-cracking beak.

- **Str:** 20 (+5) | **Dex:** 12 (+1) | **Con:** 17 (+3) | **Int:** 3 (-4) | **Wis:** 12 (+1) | **Cha:** 7 (-2)
- **Skills:** Perception +3
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft.
- **Traits:**
  - **Keen Sight and Smell:** Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight or smell.
- **Actions:**
  - **Multiattack:** Makes two attacks: one with its beak and one with its claws.
  - **Beak:** Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. Hit: 10 (1d10 + 5) piercing damage.
  - **Claws:** Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 14 (2d8 + 5) slashing damage.
