# Minotaur
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Monstrosity
> - Size:: Large
> - CR:: 3
> - AC:: 14 (natural)
> - HP:: 76 (9d10 + 27)
> - Speed:: 40 ft.
> - Tags:: #monster #dungeon-staple #monstrosity #dungeon #labyrinth

Savage bull-headed behemoths that stalk winding underground labyrinths, never losing their way while tracking interlopers with raw fury.

- **Str:** 18 (+4) | **Dex:** 11 (+0) | **Con:** 16 (+3) | **Int:** 6 (-2) | **Wis:** 16 (+3) | **Cha:** 9 (-1)
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft.
- **Traits:**
  - **Charge:** If it moves at least 10 ft. straight toward a target and hits with a gore attack, deals an extra 9 (2d8) piercing damage and DC 14 Str save or pushed 10 ft. and knocked prone.
  - **Labyrinthine Recall:** Can perfectly recall any path it has traveled.
  - **Reckless:** Can gain advantage on melee attack rolls in exchange for granting advantage to attacks against it.
- **Actions:**
  - **Greataxe:** Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 17 (2d12 + 4) slashing damage.
  - **Gore:** Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 13 (2d8 + 4) piercing damage.
