# Spy
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Humanoid
> - Size:: Medium
> - CR:: 1
> - AC:: 12
> - HP:: 27 (6d8)
> - Speed:: 30 ft.
> - Tags:: #monster #npc #humanoid #urban

An undercover operative or master of espionage skilled in gathering intelligence, tailing marks, picking locks, and silent assassinations.

- **Str:** 10 (+0) | **Dex:** 15 (+2) | **Con:** 10 (+0) | **Int:** 12 (+1) | **Wis:** 14 (+2) | **Cha:** 16 (+3)
- **Skills:** Deception +5, Insight +4, Investigation +5, Perception +6, Sleight of Hand +4, Stealth +4
- **Traits:**
  - **Cunning Action:** Can take Dash, Disengage, or Hide as a bonus action on each turn.
  - **Sneak Attack:** Extra 7 (2d6) damage once per turn when attacking with advantage or with an ally within 5 ft.
- **Actions:**
  - **Multiattack:** Makes two melee attacks.
  - **Shortsword:** Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage.
  - **Hand Crossbow:** Ranged Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, range 30/120 ft., one target. Hit: 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage.
