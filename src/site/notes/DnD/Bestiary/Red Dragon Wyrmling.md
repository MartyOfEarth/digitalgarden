# Red Dragon Wyrmling
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Dragon
> - Size:: Medium
> - CR:: 4
> - AC:: 17 (natural)
> - HP:: 75 (10d8 + 30)
> - Speed:: 30 ft., Climb 30 ft., Fly 60 ft.
> - Tags:: #monster #dragon #mountain

A newly hatched red dragon, already ferocious, vain, and greedy, instinctively amassing minor coins and incinerating small prey.

- **Str:** 19 (+4) | **Dex:** 10 (+0) | **Con:** 17 (+3) | **Int:** 12 (+1) | **Wis:** 11 (+0) | **Cha:** 15 (+2)
- **Saving Throws:** Dex +2, Con +5, Wis +2, Cha +4
- **Skills:** Perception +4, Stealth +2
- **Damage Immunities:** Fire
- **Senses:** Blindsight 10 ft., Darkvision 60 ft.
- **Actions:**
  - **Multiattack:** Makes one bite attack and one claw attack.
  - **Bite:** Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 9 (1d10 + 4) piercing damage plus 3 (1d6) fire damage.
  - **Claw:** Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 7 (1d6 + 4) slashing damage.
  - **Fire Breath (Recharge 5–6):** 15-foot cone; DC 13 Dex save or take 24 (7d6) fire damage (half on success).
