# Aboleth
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Aberration
> - Size:: Large
> - CR:: 10
> - AC:: 17
> - HP:: 135 (18d10 + 36)
> - Speed:: 10 ft., Swim 40 ft.
> - Tags:: #monster #boss #aberration #underdark #aquatic

Ancient, [[DnD/Languages/Primordial\|primordial]] horrors that ruled the multiverse before the rise of the gods. Aboleths possess perfect ancestral memories and enslave humanoids telepathically in subterranean depths.

- **Str:** 21 (+5) | **Dex:** 9 (-1) | **Con:** 15 (+2) | **Int:** 18 (+4) | **Wis:** 15 (+2) | **Cha:** 18 (+4)
- **Senses:** Darkvision 120 ft.
- **Traits:**
  - **Amphibious:** Breathe air and water.
  - **Mucous Cloud:** While underwater, surrounded by transformative mucus; anyone touching it must succeed on a DC 14 Con save or become diseased and unable to breathe air for 1d4 hours.
  - **Probing Telepathy:** If a creature communicates telepathically with it, the aboleth learns its greatest desires.
- **Actions:**
  - **Multiattack:** Makes three tentacle attacks.
  - **Tentacle:** Melee Weapon Attack: +9 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 12 (2d6 + 5) bludgeoning damage plus DC 14 Con save against a skin-rotting disease.
  - **Enslave (3/Day):** Targets one creature within 30 ft.; DC 14 Wis save or magically charmed until the aboleth dies or is freed.
- **Legendary Actions (3/Round):** Detect, Tail Swipe (+9 to hit, 15 bludgeoning), Psychic Drain (costs 2 actions).
