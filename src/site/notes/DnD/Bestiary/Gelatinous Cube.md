# Gelatinous Cube
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Ooze
> - Size:: Large
> - CR:: 2
> - AC:: 6
> - HP:: 84 (8d10 + 40)
> - Speed:: 15 ft.
> - Tags:: #monster #dungeon-staple #ooze #dungeon

A translucent, ten-foot cube of acidic slime that scours dungeon corridors clean of bones, organic waste, and unwary adventurers.

- **Str:** 14 (+2) | **Dex:** 3 (-4) | **Con:** 20 (+5) | **Int:** 1 (-5) | **Wis:** 6 (-2) | **Cha:** 1 (-5)
- **Condition Immunities:** Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Exhaustion, Prone
- **Senses:** Blindsight 60 ft.
- **Traits:**
  - **Ooze Cube:** Takes up its entire 10-foot space. Other creatures can enter the space, but doing so subjects them to Engulf.
  - **Transparent:** Hard to spot while motionless; requires a DC 15 Wis (Perception) check to notice.
- **Actions:**
  - **Pseudopod:** Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. Hit: 10 (3d6) acid damage.
  - **Engulf:** Enters the spaces of creatures. Targets must make a DC 12 Dex save; on failure, taking 10 (3d6) acid damage and becoming engulfed (Restrained, unable to breathe, takes 21 acid damage at the start of each cube turn).
