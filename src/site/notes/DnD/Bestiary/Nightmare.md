# Nightmare
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Fiend
> - Size:: Large
> - CR:: 3
> - AC:: 13
> - HP:: 68 (8d10 + 24)
> - Speed:: 60 ft.
> - Capacity:: 540 lbs
> - Tags:: #monster #mount #fiend #planar

Demonic steeds hailing from the Lower Planes, marked by coats of pitch-black hide, glowing red eyes, and manes and hooves of searing fire.

- **Str:** 18 (+4) | **Dex:** 15 (+2) | **Con:** 16 (+3) | **Int:** 10 (+0) | **Wis:** 13 (+1) | **Cha:** 15 (+2)
- **Damage Immunities:** Fire
- **Traits:**
  - **Confer Fire Resistance:** The nightmare can grant fire resistance to anyone riding it.
  - **Illumination:** Sheds bright light in a 10-foot radius and dim light for an additional 10 feet.
- **Actions:**
  - **Hooves:** Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 13 (2d8 + 4) bludgeoning damage plus 7 (2d6) fire damage.
  - **Ethereal Stride:** The nightmare and up to three willing creatures within 5 ft. magically enter the Ethereal Plane from the Material Plane, or vice versa.
