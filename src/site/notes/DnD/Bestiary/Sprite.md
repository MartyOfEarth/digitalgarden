# Sprite
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Fey
> - Size:: Tiny
> - CR:: 1/4
> - AC:: 15 (leather)
> - HP:: 2 (1d4)
> - Speed:: 10 ft., Fly 40 ft.
> - Tags:: #monster #familiar #pact-familiar #fey

A fierce, honorable fey warrior equipped with a miniature sword and shortbow, capable of sensing creatures' true alignment and emotions with a touch.

- **Str:** 3 (-4) | **Dex:** 18 (+4) | **Con:** 10 (+0) | **Int:** 14 (+2) | **Wis:** 13 (+1) | **Cha:** 11 (+0)
- **Skills:** Perception +3, Stealth +8
- **Actions:**
  - **Longsword:** Melee Weapon Attack: +2 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 1 slashing damage.
  - **Shortbow:** Ranged Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, range 40/160 ft., one target. Hit: 1 piercing damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 10 Con save or become Poisoned for 1 minute (falls unconscious if the roll is 5 or lower).
  - **Heart Sight:** Touch a creature to know its current emotional state and exact alignment (unless protected against divination).
  - **Invisibility:** Magically turns invisible.
