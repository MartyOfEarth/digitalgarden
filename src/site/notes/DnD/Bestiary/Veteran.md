# Veteran

Hardened mercenary soldiers, seasoned bodyguards, and elite infantry officers who have survived countless campaigns through martial prowess.

- **Str:** 16 (+3) | **Dex:** 13 (+1) | **Con:** 14 (+2) | **Int:** 10 (+0) | **Wis:** 11 (+0) | **Cha:** 10 (+0)
