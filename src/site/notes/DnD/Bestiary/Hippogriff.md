# Hippogriff
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Monstrosity
> - Size:: Large
> - CR:: 1
> - AC:: 11
> - HP:: 19 (3d10 + 3)
> - Speed:: 40 ft.
> - Capacity:: 510 lbs
> - Tags:: #monster #mount #flying #aerial

A hybrid creature with the forelegs, wings, and head of a giant eagle and the hindquarters of a horse. Easier to train and domesticate than a predatory griffon.

- **Str:** 17 (+3) | **Dex:** 13 (+1) | **Con:** 13 (+1) | **Int:** 2 (-4) | **Wis:** 12 (+1) | **Cha:** 8 (-1)
- **Traits:**
  - **Keen Sight:** Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight.
- **Actions:**
  - **Multiattack:** The hippogriff makes two attacks: one with its beak and one with its claws.
  - **Beak:** Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 7 (1d8 + 3) piercing damage.
  - **Claws:** Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 10 (2d6 + 3) slashing damage.
