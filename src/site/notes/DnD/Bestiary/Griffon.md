# Griffon
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Monstrosity
> - Size:: Large
> - CR:: 2
> - AC:: 12
> - HP:: 59 (7d10 + 21)
> - Speed:: 30 ft.
> - Capacity:: 540 lbs
> - Tags:: #monster #mount #flying #aerial

Fierce carnivores combining the body and hindquarters of a lion with the wings, forelegs, and head of a giant eagle. Trained from an egg, griffons make lethal aerial cavalry mounts.

- **Str:** 18 (+4) | **Dex:** 15 (+2) | **Con:** 16 (+3) | **Int:** 2 (-4) | **Wis:** 13 (+1) | **Cha:** 8 (-1)
- **Traits:**
  - **Keen Sight:** Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight.
- **Actions:**
  - **Multiattack:** The griffon makes two attacks: one with its beak and one with its claws.
  - **Beak:** Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 8 (1d8 + 4) piercing damage.
  - **Claws:** Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 11 (2d6 + 4) slashing damage.
