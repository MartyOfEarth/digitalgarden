# Brown Bear
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Beast
> - Size:: Large
> - CR:: 1
> - AC:: 11 (natural)
> - HP:: 34 (4d10 + 12)
> - Speed:: 40 ft., Climb 30 ft.
> - Tags:: #monster #summon #wildshape #forest #beast

A hulking apex predator of temperate forests. The premier combat Wild Shape for Circle of the Moon [[DnD/Classes/Druid\|Druids]] at 2nd level, offering heavy multiattack damage and a large HP pool.

- **Str:** 19 (+4) | **Dex:** 10 (+0) | **Con:** 16 (+3) | **Int:** 2 (-4) | **Wis:** 13 (+1) | **Cha:** 7 (-2)
- **Skills:** Perception +3
- **Traits:**
  - **Keen Smell:** Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on smell.
- **Actions:**
  - **Multiattack:** Makes two attacks: one with its bite and one with its claws.
  - **Bite:** Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 8 (1d8 + 4) piercing damage.
  - **Claws:** Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 11 (2d6 + 4) slashing damage.
