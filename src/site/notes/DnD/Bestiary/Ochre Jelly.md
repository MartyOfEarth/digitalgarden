# Ochre Jelly
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Ooze
> - Size:: Large
> - CR:: 2
> - AC:: 8
> - HP:: 45 (6d10 + 12)
> - Speed:: 10 ft., Climb 10 ft.
> - Tags:: #monster #dungeon-staple #ooze #dungeon

A yellowish amorphous sludge that slips under doors and along stone ceilings, dissolving organic matter and dividing when struck by weapons or lightning.

- **Str:** 15 (+2) | **Dex:** 6 (-2) | **Con:** 14 (+2) | **Int:** 2 (-4) | **Wis:** 6 (-2) | **Cha:** 1 (-5)
- **Damage Immunities:** Lightning, Slashing
- **Condition Immunities:** Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Exhaustion, Prone
- **Senses:** Blindsight 60 ft.
- **Traits:**
  - **Amorphous:** Move through a space as narrow as 1 inch wide without squeezing.
  - **Spider Climb:** Climb difficult surfaces without an ability check.
- **Actions:**
  - **Pseudopod:** Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 9 (2d6 + 2) bludgeoning damage plus 3 (1d6) acid damage.
- **Reactions:**
  - **Split:** If a Large or Medium jelly takes lightning or slashing damage, it splits into two new jellies if it has at least 10 HP.
