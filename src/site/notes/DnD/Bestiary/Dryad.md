# Dryad
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Fey
> - Size:: Medium
> - CR:: 1
> - AC:: 11 (16 with barkskin)
> - HP:: 22 (5d8)
> - Speed:: 30 ft.
> - Tags:: #monster #fey #forest

A shy, solitary fey spirit magically bonded to an ancient tree, defending the heart of primeval woodlands through charms and nature magic.

- **Str:** 10 (+0) | **Dex:** 12 (+1) | **Con:** 11 (+0) | **Int:** 14 (+2) | **Wis:** 15 (+2) | **Cha:** 18 (+4)
- **Skills:** Perception +4, Stealth +5
- **Traits:**
  - **Tree Stride:** Once on its turn, can use 10 ft. of movement to step into a living tree and emerge from another tree within 60 ft.
  - **Innate Spellcasting:** At will: *Druidcraft*. 3/day each: *Entangle*, *Goodberry*. 1/day each: *Barkskin*, *Pass without Trace*, *Shillelagh*.
  - **Magic Resistance:** Advantage on saves against spells and magical effects.
- **Actions:**
  - **Club:** Melee Weapon Attack: +2 to hit (+6 with shillelagh), reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 2 (1d4) bludgeoning damage, or 8 (1d8 + 4) with shillelagh.
  - **Fey Charm:** One humanoid or beast within 30 ft. must succeed on a DC 14 Wis save or be magically charmed for 24 hours.
