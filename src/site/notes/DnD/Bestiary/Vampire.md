# Vampire
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Undead
> - Size:: Medium
> - CR:: 13
> - AC:: 16 (natural)
> - HP:: 144 (17d8 + 68)
> - Speed:: 30 ft.
> - Tags:: #monster #boss #legendary #undead #urban

Aristocratic lords of the night who rule cursed gothic domains, commanding wolves and bats while preying on the blood of living mortals.

- **Str:** 18 (+4) | **Dex:** 18 (+4) | **Con:** 18 (+4) | **Int:** 17 (+3) | **Wis:** 15 (+2) | **Cha:** 18 (+4)
- **Damage Resistances:** Necrotic; nonmagical physical attacks
- **Senses:** Darkvision 120 ft.
- **Traits:**
  - **Legendary Resistance (3/Day):** Succeeds on a failed save.
  - **Regeneration:** Regains 20 HP at start of turn if not in sunlight or running water.
  - **Spider Climb:** Climb difficult surfaces and ceilings without checks.
  - **Vampire Weaknesses:** Forbiddance, Running Water, Stake to the Heart, Sunlight Hypersensitivity (takes 20 radiant damage at start of turn).
- **Actions:**
  - **Multiattack:** Makes two attacks, only one of which can be a bite attack.
  - **Unarmed Strike:** Melee Weapon Attack: +9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. Hit: 8 (1d8 + 4) bludgeoning damage plus Grappled (escape DC 18).
  - **Bite:** Melee Weapon Attack: +9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one willing or grappled/incapacitated creature. Hit: 7 (1d6 + 4) piercing plus 10 (3d6) necrotic damage.
- **Legendary Actions (3/Round):** Move (no opportunity attacks), Unarmed Strike, Bite (costs 2 actions).
