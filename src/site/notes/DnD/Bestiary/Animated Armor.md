# Animated Armor
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Construct
> - Size:: Medium
> - CR:: 1
> - AC:: 18 (natural)
> - HP:: 33 (6d8 + 6)
> - Speed:: 25 ft.
> - Tags:: #monster #minion #construct #dungeon

An empty suit of plate armor magically animated to guard ancient vaults, [[DnD/Classes/Wizard\|wizards]]' studies, and sealed treasure chambers.

- **Str:** 14 (+2) | **Dex:** 11 (+0) | **Con:** 13 (+1) | **Int:** 1 (-5) | **Wis:** 3 (-4) | **Cha:** 1 (-5)
- **Damage Immunities:** Poison, Psychic
- **Condition Immunities:** Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned
- **Senses:** Blindsight 60 ft. (blind beyond)
- **Traits:**
  - **Antimagic Susceptibility:** Incapacitated while in an antimagic field; falls unconscious if targeted by *Dispel Magic* (Con save against caster DC).
  - **False Appearance:** Indistinguishable from normal suit of armor while motionless.
- **Actions:**
  - **Multiattack:** Makes two melee slam attacks.
  - **Slam:** Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 5 (1d6 + 2) bludgeoning damage.
