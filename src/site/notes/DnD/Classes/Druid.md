# Druid
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - hit_die :: d8
> - primary_ability:: Wisdom
> - Saves:: Intelligence, Wisdom

- **Armor & Weapon Proficiencies:** Light armor, medium armor, shields, simple weapons
- **Overview:** A guardian of nature and balance who wields the raw primal elements of earth, sky, and sea, able to take the shape of untamed beasts.
- **Key Features:**
  - **Primal Order:** Choose *Magician* (bonus cantrip, Nature check bonus) or *Warden* (medium armor, martial weapons).
  - **Wild Shape:** Magically assume the form of beasts for combat, stealth, utility, or flight.
  - **Wild Companion:** Summon a temporary familiar using a use of Wild Shape.
  - **Spellcasting:** Wisdom-based prepared full caster specializing in battlefield control, elemental damage, and healing.
- **Subclasses:** Circle of the Land, Circle of the Moon, Circle of the Sea, Circle of Spores.

[[DnD/Classes/Druid\|Druids]] belong to ancient orders that call on the forces of nature. Harnessing the magic of animals, plants, and the four elements, [[DnD/Classes/Druid\|Druids]] heal, transform into animals, and wield elemental destruction.

Revering nature above all, individual [[DnD/Classes/Druid\|Druids]] gain their magic from nature, a nature deity, or both, and they typically unite with other [[DnD/Classes/Druid\|Druids]] to perform rites that mark the passage of the seasons and other natural cycles.

[[DnD/Classes/Druid\|Druids]] are concerned with the delicate ecological balance that sustains plant and animal life and with the need for people to live in harmony with nature. [[DnD/Classes/Druid\|Druids]] often guard sacred sites or watch over regions of unspoiled nature, but when a significant danger arises, [[DnD/Classes/Druid\|Druids]] take a more active role as adventurers who combat the threat.
