# Wizard
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - hit_die:: d6
> - primary_ability:: Intelligence
> - Saves:: Intelligence, Wisdom

- **Armor & Weapon Proficiencies:** Daggers, darts, slings, quarterstaffs, light crossbows
- **Overview:** The supreme scholarly practitioner of the arcane arts, learning spells through meticulous study, deciphering scrolls, and maintaining an ever-expanding spellbook.
- **Key Features:**
  - **Spellbook:** Record an unlimited number of arcane spells found across scrolls and captured books.
  - **Ritual Casting:** Cast any ritual spell from your spellbook without expending a spell slot or having it prepared.
  - **Arcane Recovery:** Regain expended spell slots during a short rest once per day.
  - **Scholar:** Gain Expertise in one academic skill (Arcana, History, Nature, or Religion).
- **Subclasses:** Abjurer, Diviner, Evoker, Illusionist.
