# Sorcerer
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - hit_die :: d6
> - primary_ability:: Charisma
> - Saves:: Constitution, Charisma

- **Armor & Weapon Proficiencies:** Daggers, darts, slings, quarterstaffs, light crossbows
- **Overview:** A spellcaster whose magic is not studied or granted, but an innate, roiling power stamped into their very bloodline, soul, or cosmic origin.
- **Key Features:**
  - **Innate Sorcery:** Bonus action to unleash an aura of surging magic for 1 minute, giving advantage on spell attack rolls and increasing spell save DC.
  - **Font of Magic & Sorcery Points:** Convert spell slots into points and points into spell slots on the fly.
  - **Metamagic:** Alter spells as you cast them (e.g., Quickened Spell, Twinned Spell, Subtle Spell, Heightened Spell).
  - **Spellcasting:** Charisma-based full spellcaster channeling raw arcane energy.
- **Subclasses:** Aberrant Sorcery, Clockwork Sorcery, [[DnD/Languages/Draconic\|Draconic]] Sorcery, Wild Magic Sorcery.
