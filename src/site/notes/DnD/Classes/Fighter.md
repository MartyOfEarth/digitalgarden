# Fighter
- **Primary Ability:** Strength or Dexterity | **Hit Die:** d10 | **Saves:** Strength, Constitution
- **Armor & Weapon Proficiencies:** All armor, shields, simple weapons, martial weapons
- **Overview:** A disciplined master of all forms of martial combat, equipment, and battlefield tactics, unequaled in sheer weapon technique and endurance.
- **Key Features:**
  - **Fighting Style:** Specialize in combat forms such as Archery, Defense, Dueling, or Great Weapon Fighting.
  - **Second Wind:** Bonus action to regain a pool of hit points during battle.
  - **Action Surge:** Push past normal limits to take one additional action on your turn.
  - **Weapon Mastery:** Master properties on multiple weapons, switching techniques fluidly between strikes.
  - **Extra Attack:** Attack up to four times per attack action at higher levels.
- **Subclasses:** Battle Master, Champion, Eldritch Knight, Psi Warrior.
