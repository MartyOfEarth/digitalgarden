# Paladin
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - hit_die :: d10
> - primary_ability:: Strength, Charisma
> - Saves:: Wisdom, Charisma

- **Armor & Weapon Proficiencies:** All armor, shields, simple weapons, martial weapons
- **Overview:** A holy warrior bound by a sacred oath, standing as an unyielding wall against darkness, clad in heavy plate and fueled by radiant smites.
- **Key Features:**
  - **Lay on Hands:** Heal creatures from a pool of restorative power equal to 5 × Paladin level.
  - **Paladin's Smite:** Infuse melee attacks with radiant energy (*Divine Smite* and oath smite spells).
  - **Spellcasting:** Charisma-based prepared half-caster using a holy symbol.
  - **Aura of Protection:** Radiate a protective aura giving you and nearby allies a bonus to all saving throws equal to your Charisma modifier.
- **Subclasses:** Oath of Devotion, Oath of Glory, Oath of the Ancients, Oath of Vengeance.
