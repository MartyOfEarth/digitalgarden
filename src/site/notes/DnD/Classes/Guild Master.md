> [!infobox] Statblock
> - hit_die :: d8
> - primary_ability:: Charisma
> - Saves:: Intelligence, Charisma

- **Armor & Weapon Proficiencies:** Light armor, medium armor, simple weapons, rapiers, hand crossbows
- **Overview:** Shrewd organizers, networkers, and tacticians who wield power through institutional wealth, specialized contracts, and coordinated retinues rather than raw personal brawn.

- **Key Features:**
 - **Guild Directives:** Use an action or bonus action to issue commands that grant allies temporary buffs, repositioning options, or bonus damage on coordinated strikes.
 - **Charter Magic:** Charisma- or Intelligence-based prepared spellcasting focused on commerce, communication, warding, and enchantment, channeled through a seal of office, guild signet, or ledger.
 - **Underwriter’s Ledger:** Invest guild capital during a rest to requisition rare equipment, secure safe passage, or grant retainers and party members custom logistical boons.
 - **Master of Retainers:** Summon or maintain a small retinue of guild hirelings (couriers, bodyguards, or specialist artisans) to handle tasks, scout, or provide battlefield support.

**Subclasses:**
- **Syndicate Broker:** Specializes in illicit markets, espionage, bribes, and underground extortion.
- **High Factor (Merchant Prince):** Commands vast fiscal reserves, supply lines, mercenaries, and magical trade goods.
- **Grand Architect:** Directs public works, fortifications, defensive wards, and structural engineering.
- **Archivist Provost:** Oversees contract law, arcane libraries, forbidden registries, and binding magical pacts.
