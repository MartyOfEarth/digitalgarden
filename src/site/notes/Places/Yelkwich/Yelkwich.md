## About
[[Places/Yelkwich/Yelkwich\|Yelkwich]] is a bustling town situated along the rushing banks of the [[Places/POI/Serpentine River\|Serpentine River]] and carved directly into the sheer cliffs of the [[Places/POI/Ironvein Mountains\|Ironvein Mountains]]. It is a capital city, and considered a titan of trade and history.

## History
Founded over five hundred years ago in the bloody crucible of the [[History/Great Racial Wars\|Great Racial Wars]], the settlement began as a desperate sanctuary discovered by a visionary [[History/Dragonborne\|Dragonborne]]. Tucked away in the shadow of the peaks, the [[Places/POI/Ironvein Mountains\|mountain]] provided vast, rich veins of ore for forging weapons, while the [[Places/POI/Serpentine River\|river]] offered a steady food supply and a direct gateway to the open sea. What began as a hidden fortress built for survival soon transformed into the continent’s most powerful engine of commerce.

## Now
Today, [[Places/Yelkwich/Yelkwich\|Yelkwich]] is the beating heart of global trade. Adventurers from across the realm flock to its legendary guildhalls seeking fame, fortune, and high-paying contracts. Where heroes gather, gold follows—drawing ambitious merchants who line the streets with rare goods, exotic beasts, and enchanted wares.
