Deep within the [[Places/POI/Ironvein Mountains\|mountain’s]] hollowed plank lies a secret metropolis reserved strictly for high-ranking shopmasters and lifelong citizens. Here, wooden bridges suspend over subterranean neighborhoods and private businesses carved out of the living rock. Buried in the deepest level lies [[Places/Yelkwich/The Vael\|The Vael]].
