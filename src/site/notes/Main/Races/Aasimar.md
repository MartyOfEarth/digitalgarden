## Aasimar
- **Type:** Humanoid | **Size:** Medium or Small | **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **Overview:** Mortals touched by [[Main/Languages/Celestial\|celestial]] power from the Upper Planes, often bearing glowing eyes, metallic hair, or subtle halos.
- **Characteristics & Traits:**
  - **[[Main/Languages/Celestial\|Celestial]] Resistance:** Resistance to necrotic and radiant damage.
  - **Darkvision:** 60 ft.
  - **Healing Hands:** Can touch a creature to restore hit points equal to a roll of d4s scaling with proficiency bonus.
  - **Light Bearer:** Innately knows the *Light* cantrip.
  - **[[Main/Languages/Celestial\|Celestial]] Revelation (3rd Level):** Once per long rest, can transform for 1 minute to sprout radiant wings, emanate searing light, or frighten nearby foes.
