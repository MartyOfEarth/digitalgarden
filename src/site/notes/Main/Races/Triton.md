## Triton
- **Type:** Humanoid | **Size:** Medium | **Speed:** 30 ft., Swim 30 ft.
- **Overview:** Noble aquatic guardians from the Elemental Plane of Water who defend oceanic depths from leviathans.
- **Characteristics & Traits:**
  - **Amphibious:** Can breathe air and water.
  - **Control Air and Water:** Can cast *Fog Cloud*, *Gust of Wind* (at 3rd level), and *Water Walk* (at 5th level).
  - **Darkvision:** 60 ft.
  - **Emissary of the Sea:** Aquatic beasts can understand their speech.
  - **Cold Resistance:** Resistance to cold damage.
