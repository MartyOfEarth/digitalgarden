## Human
- **Type:** Humanoid | **Size:** Medium or Small | **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **Overview:** The most adaptable, ambitious, and widely populated species, found thriving across every terrain and trade.
- **Characteristics & Traits:**
  - **Resourceful:** Gains Heroic Inspiration upon finishing a long rest.
  - **Skillful:** Proficiency in one skill of choice.
  - **Versatile:** Possesses an extra 1st-level Origin feat (e.g., Alert, Magic Initiate, Tough).

### Lore & Culture
Found throughout the multiverse, humans are as varied as they are numerous. Their ambition and resourcefulness are commended, respected, and feared across many worlds.

Humans are immensely diverse in appearance and culture, revering a vast pantheon of gods. While scholars debate their true origin, historical accounts frequently place early human gatherings in Sigil, the city at the center of the multiverse where the [[Main/Languages/Common\|Common language]] originated. From there, humans expanded across the cosmos, carrying their cosmopolitan nature to every world.
