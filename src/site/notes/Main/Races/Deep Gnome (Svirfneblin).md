## Deep Gnome (Svirfneblin)
- **Type:** Humanoid ([[Main/Races/Gnome\|Gnome]]) | **Size:** Small | **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **Overview:** Hardy, gray-skinned subterranean [[Main/Races/Gnome\|gnomes]] dwelling in the deepest recesses of the Underdark.
- **Characteristics & Traits:**
  - **Superior Darkvision:** 120 ft.
  - **[[Main/Languages/Gnomish\|Gnomish]] Cunning:** Advantage on all Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma saving throws against spells.
  - **Svirfneblin Camouflage:** Advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks to hide in rocky terrain.
  - **Gift of the Svirfneblin:** Can cast *Disguise Self* (at 3rd level) and *Nondetection* (at 5th level) once per long rest.
