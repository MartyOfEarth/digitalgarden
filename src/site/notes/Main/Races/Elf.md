## Elf
- **Type:** Humanoid | **Size:** Medium | **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **Overview:** Graceful, long-lived peoples deeply intertwined with magic, nature, and otherworldly realms.
- **Characteristics & Traits:**
  - **Darkvision:** 60 ft. | **Keen Senses:** Proficiency in Perception.
  - **Fey Ancestry:** Advantage on saving throws against being Charmed.
  - **Trance:** Meditates for 4 hours instead of sleeping to gain a long rest.
  - **Lineages:** High Elves (know a wizard cantrip and *Detect Magic*), Wood Elves (+5 ft speed, *Pass Without Trace*), or Drow (120 ft darkvision, *Darkness*).
