# Monk
- **Primary Ability:** Dexterity, Wisdom | **Hit Die:** d8 | **Saves:** Strength, Dexterity
- **Armor & Weapon Proficiencies:** Simple weapons, martial weapons with the Light property
- **Overview:** A martial artist who channels an inner reservoir of metaphysical focus (Ki / Focus Points) to turn their body into a lethal weapon and dodge danger effortlessly.
- **Key Features:**
  - **Martial Arts:** Use Dexterity for unarmed strikes and monk weapons; bonus action unarmed strikes; scaling strike damage die.
  - **Unarmored Defense:** AC equals 10 + Dex modifier + Wis modifier without armor or shields.
  - **Monk's Focus:** Spend focus points on *Flurry of Blows*, *Patient Defense*, and *Step of the Wind*.
  - **Deflect Attacks:** Use a reaction to reduce or catch incoming physical and elemental ranged/melee damage.
  - **Stunning Strike:** Interfere with an enemy's bodily energy to stun them until the start of your next turn.
- **Subclasses:** Warrior of Mercy, Warrior of Shadow, Warrior of the Elements, Warrior of the Open Hand.
