# Ranger
- **Primary Ability:** Dexterity, Wisdom | **Hit Die:** d10 | **Saves:** Strength, Dexterity
- **Armor & Weapon Proficiencies:** Light armor, medium armor, shields, simple weapons, martial weapons
- **Overview:** A peerless scout and wilderness hunter who blends tactical martial archery and dual-wielding with primal nature magic to track and eliminate quarry.
- **Key Features:**
  - **Deft Explorer:** Gain Expertise in one skill, plus extra [[Main/Languages/Languages\|languages]], swimming, and climbing speeds.
  - **Favored Enemy:** Always have *Hunter's Mark* prepared, casting it free multiple times per day.
  - **Weapon Mastery:** Exploit specialized mastery weapon traits on bows, blades, and polearms.
  - **Spellcasting:** Wisdom-based prepared half-caster specializing in tracking, stealth, and archery enhancements.
- **Subclasses:** Beast Master, Fey Wanderer, Gloom Stalker, Hunter.

Far from bustling cities, amid the trees of trackless forests and across wide plains, [[Main/Classes/Ranger\|Rangers]] keep their unending watch in the wilderness. [[Main/Classes/Ranger\|Rangers]] learn to track their quarry as a predator does, moving stealthily through the wilds and hiding themselves in brush and rubble.

Thanks to their connection with nature, [[Main/Classes/Ranger\|Rangers]] can also cast spells that harness primal powers of the wilderness. A [[Main/Classes/Ranger\|Ranger’s]] talents and magic are honed with deadly focus to protect the world from the ravages of monsters and tyrants.
