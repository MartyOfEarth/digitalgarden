# Rogue
- **Primary Ability:** Dexterity | **Hit Die:** d8 | **Saves:** Dexterity, Intelligence
- **Armor & Weapon Proficiencies:** Light armor, simple weapons, martial weapons with the Finesse or Light property
- **Overview:** A cunning scoundrel or precision assassin who prioritizes stealth, agility, and precision strikes over brute force to exploit enemy vulnerabilities.
- **Key Features:**
  - **Expertise:** Double proficiency bonus on selected skills (e.g., Stealth, Thieves' Tools, Sleight of Hand).
  - **Sneak Attack:** Deal bonus d6s of precision damage when you have advantage or an ally is adjacent to your target.
  - **Cunning Action:** Take Dash, Disengage, or Hide as a bonus action on every turn.
  - **Cunning Strike:** Sacrifice Sneak Attack damage dice to inflict debuffs like poison, tripping, or withdrawing.
  - **Uncanny Dodge & Evasion:** Halve incoming attack damage on reaction; take zero damage instead of half on successful Dex saves.
- **Subclasses:** Arcane Trickster, Assassin, Soulknife, Thief.
