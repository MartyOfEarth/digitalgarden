# Warlock
- **Primary Ability:** Charisma | **Hit Die:** d8 | **Saves:** Wisdom, Charisma
- **Armor & Weapon Proficiencies:** Light armor, simple weapons
- **Overview:** An arcane seeker bound by a supernatural pact with an otherworldly patron—be it an archfey, fiend, great old one, or [[Main/Languages/Celestial\|celestial]]—wielding short-rest magic and eerie gifts.
- **Key Features:**
  - **Eldritch Invocations:** Customize your character with supernatural gifts (e.g., *Agonizing Blast*, *Armor of Shadows*, devil's sight, at-will spells).
  - **Pact Magic:** Cast spells at maximum spell level using a small pool of slots that fully recover on a short rest.
  - **Pact Boon:** Choose your pact manifest: *Pact of the Blade* (conjure magical weapons), *Pact of the Tome* (extra cantrips/rituals), or *Pact of the Chain* (enhanced familiars).
  - **Magical Cunning:** Short 1-minute ritual once per day to recover half your spent pact slots.
- **Subclasses:** Archfey Patron, [[Main/Languages/Celestial\|Celestial]] Patron, Fiend Patron, Great Old One Patron.
