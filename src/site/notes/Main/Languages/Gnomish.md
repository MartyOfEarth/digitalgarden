**Script:** Chisel

**Summary:** Gnomish [[Main/Languages/Languages\|language]] developed alongside [[Main/Languages/Dwarvish\|dwarvish]], though segued into its own complex [[Main/Languages/Languages\|language]] as gnomes became more technically minded.

**Sounds:** Gnomish is a less guttural, more chittery [[Main/Languages/Languages\|language]], and is spoken a great deal faster than [[Main/Languages/Dwarvish\|dwarvish]] and most other [[Main/Languages/Languages\|languages]].

**Appearance:** Gnomish writing is designed to convey a great deal of information in a short space. The [[Main/Languages/Languages\|language]] contains thousands of characters that interlink with each other, making a gnomish text look almost like a blueprint.
