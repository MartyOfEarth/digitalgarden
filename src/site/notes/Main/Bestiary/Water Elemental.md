# Water Elemental
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Elemental
> - Size:: Large
> - CR:: 5
> - AC:: 14 (natural)
> - HP:: 114 (12d10 + 48)
> - Speed:: 30 ft., Swim 90 ft.
> - Tags:: #monster #summon #planar #elemental

A cresting wave of animate water summoned from the Elemental Plane of Water. Capable of drowning and engulfing multiple enemies at once on the battlefield.

- **Str:** 18 (+4) | **Dex:** 14 (+2) | **Con:** 18 (+4) | **Int:** 5 (-3) | **Wis:** 10 (+0) | **Cha:** 8 (-1)
- **Damage Resistances:** Acid; nonmagical physical damage
- **Damage Immunities:** Poison
- **Condition Immunities:** Exhaustion, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained, Unconscious
- **Traits:**
  - **Water Form:** Move through spaces as narrow as 1 inch without squeezing. Can enter and stop in a hostile creature's space.
- **Actions:**
  - **Multiattack:** Makes two slam attacks.
  - **Slam:** Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 13 (2d8 + 4) bludgeoning damage.
  - **Whelm (Recharge 4–6):** Each creature in the elemental's space must make a DC 15 Strength save. On failure, taking 13 (2d8 + 4) bludgeoning damage, Grappled (escape DC 14), Restrained, and unable to breathe unless it can breathe water.
