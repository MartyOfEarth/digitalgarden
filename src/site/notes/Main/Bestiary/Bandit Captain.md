# Bandit Captain
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Humanoid
> - Size:: Medium
> - CR:: 2
> - AC:: 15 (studded leather)
> - HP:: 65 (10d8 + 20)
> - Speed:: 30 ft.
> - Tags:: #monster #npc #humanoid #urban #forest

The ruthless, charismatic leader of an outlaw gang, experienced in dirty fighting and commanding highwaymen.

- **Str:** 15 (+2) | **Dex:** 16 (+3) | **Con:** 14 (+2) | **Int:** 14 (+2) | **Wis:** 11 (+0) | **Cha:** 14 (+2)
- **Saving Throws:** Str +4, Dex +5, Wis +2
- **Skills:** Athletics +4, Deception +4
- **Actions:**
  - **Multiattack:** Makes three melee attacks: two with its scimitar and one with its dagger. Or two ranged attacks with daggers.
  - **Scimitar:** Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 6 (1d6 + 3) slashing damage.
  - **Dagger:** Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one target. Hit: 5 (1d4 + 3) piercing damage.
- **Reactions:**
  - **Parry:** Adds 2 to its AC against one melee attack that would hit it.
