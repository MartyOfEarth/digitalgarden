# Knight
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Humanoid
> - Size:: Medium
> - CR:: 3
> - AC:: 18 (plate)
> - HP:: 52 (8d8 + 16)
> - Speed:: 30 ft.
> - Tags:: #monster #npc #humanoid #urban #military

Armored champions sworn to feudal chivalry, noble houses, or religious orders, skilled in defensive maneuvers and battlefield command.

- **Str:** 16 (+3) | **Dex:** 11 (+0) | **Con:** 14 (+2) | **Int:** 11 (+0) | **Wis:** 11 (+0) | **Cha:** 15 (+2)
- **Saving Throws:** Con +4, Wis +2
- **Traits:**
  - **Brave:** Advantage on saving throws against being Frightened.
- **Actions:**
  - **Multiattack:** Makes two melee attacks.
  - **Greatsword:** Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 10 (2d6 + 3) slashing damage.
  - **Leadership (Recharges after Short/Long Rest):** For 1 minute, can utter a command whenever an ally within 30 ft. makes an attack or save, adding a d4 to the roll.
- **Reactions:**
  - **Parry:** Adds 2 to its AC against one melee attack that would hit it.
