# [[Main/Bestiary/Bugbear\|Bugbear]]
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Humanoid
> - Size:: Medium
> - CR:: 1
> - AC:: 16 (hide, shield)
> - HP:: 27 (5d8 + 5)
> - Speed:: 30 ft.

Large, stealthy goblinoid brutes that lurk in caves and ruined keeps, ambushing unsuspecting targets from shadows with crushing morningstars.

- **Str:** 15 (+2) | **Dex:** 14 (+2) | **Con:** 13 (+1) | **Int:** 8 (-1) | **Wis:** 11 (+0) | **Cha:** 9 (-1)
- **Skills:** Stealth +6, Survival +2
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft.
- **Traits:**
  - **Brute:** A melee weapon deals one extra die of damage when the [[Main/Bestiary/Bugbear\|bugbear]] hits with it (included in attacks).
  - **Surprise Attack:** If the [[Main/Bestiary/Bugbear\|bugbear]] surprises a creature and hits it with an attack on the first round, it deals an extra 7 (2d6) damage.
- **Actions:**
  - **Morningstar:** Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 11 (2d8 + 2) piercing damage.
  - **Javelin:** Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 30/120 ft., one target. Hit: 9 (2d6 + 2) piercing damage.
