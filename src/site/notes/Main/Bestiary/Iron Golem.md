# Iron Golem
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Construct
> - Size:: Large
> - CR:: 16
> - AC:: 20 (natural)
> - HP:: 210 (20d10 + 100)
> - Speed:: 30 ft.
> - Tags:: #monster #boss #construct #dungeon

A towering juggernaut forged of solid iron, completely impervious to mundane steel, magic, and fire, wielding a massive sword and poisonous breath.

- **Str:** 24 (+7) | **Dex:** 9 (-1) | **Con:** 20 (+5) | **Int:** 3 (-4) | **Wis:** 11 (+0) | **Cha:** 1 (-5)
- **Damage Immunities:** Fire, Poison, Psychic; nonmagical physical damage not made with adamantine
- **Condition Immunities:** Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned
- **Senses:** Darkvision 120 ft.
- **Traits:**
  - **Fire Absorption:** Whenever subjected to fire damage, takes no damage and regains HP equal to the fire damage dealt.
  - **Immutable Form:** Immune to any spell or effect that would alter its form.
  - **Magic Resistance:** Advantage on saving throws against spells and magical effects.
  - **Magic Weapons:** Weapon attacks are magical.
- **Actions:**
  - **Multiattack:** Makes two melee attacks.
  - **Slam:** Melee Weapon Attack: +13 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 20 (3d8 + 7) bludgeoning damage.
  - **Sword:** Melee Weapon Attack: +13 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 23 (3d10 + 7) slashing damage.
  - **Poison Breath (Recharge 6):** 15-foot cone; DC 19 Con save or take 45 (10d8) poison damage (half on success).
