Disciplined, strategic martial soldiers who fight in coordinated phalanxes and demand total order and obedience across their ranks.

- **Str:** 13 (+1) | **Dex:** 12 (+1) | **Con:** 12 (+1) | **Int:** 10 (+0) | **Wis:** 10 (+0) | **Cha:** 9 (-1)
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft.
- **Traits:**
  - **Martial Advantage:** Once per turn, deals an extra 7 (2d6) damage to a creature it hits with a weapon attack if that target is within 5 ft. of an ally that isn't incapacitated.
- **Actions:**

  - **Longbow:** Ranged Weapon Attack: +3 to hit, range 150/600 ft., one target. Hit: 5 (1d8 + 1) piercing damage.
