# Troll
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Giant
> - Size:: Large
> - CR:: 5
> - AC:: 15 (natural)
> - HP:: 84 (8d10 + 40)
> - Speed:: 30 ft.
> - Tags:: #monster #dungeon-staple #giant #swamp

Voracious carnivores endowed with legendary regenerative powers, whose flesh only ceases healing when scorched with acid or flame.

- **Str:** 18 (+4) | **Dex:** 13 (+1) | **Con:** 20 (+5) | **Int:** 7 (-2) | **Wis:** 9 (-1) | **Cha:** 7 (-2)
- **Skills:** Perception +2
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft.
- **Traits:**
  - **Keen Smell:** Advantage on Perception checks relying on smell.
  - **Regeneration:** Regains 10 hit points at the start of its turn unless it took acid or fire damage since its last turn.
- **Actions:**
  - **Multiattack:** Makes three attacks: one with its bite and two with its claws.
  - **Bite:** Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 7 (1d6 + 4) piercing damage.
  - **Claw:** Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 11 (2d6 + 4) slashing damage.
