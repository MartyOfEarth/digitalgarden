# Bat
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Beast
> - Size:: Tiny
> - CR:: 0
> - AC:: 12
> - HP:: 1 (1d4 - 1)
> - Speed:: 5 ft., Fly 30 ft.
> - Tags:: #monster #familiar #beast

A tiny nocturnal mammal capable of precise navigation and scouting in pitch darkness via echolocation. A popular familiar choice for dark dungeons and caves.

- **Str:** 2 (-4) | **Dex:** 15 (+2) | **Con:** 8 (-1) | **Int:** 2 (-4) | **Wis:** 12 (+1) | **Cha:** 4 (-3)
- **Senses:** Blindsight 60 ft.
- **Traits:**
  - **Echolocation:** The bat can't use its blindsight while deafened.
  - **Keen Hearing:** Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing.
- **Actions:**
  - **Bite:** Melee Weapon Attack: +0 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. Hit: 1 piercing damage.
