# Quasit
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Fiend
> - Size:: Tiny
> - CR:: 1
> - AC:: 13
> - HP:: 7 (3d4)
> - Speed:: 40 ft.
> - Tags:: #monster #familiar #pact-familiar #fiend

A chaotic demon born in the Abyss serving spellcasters who crave chaos. Quasits turn invisible, scare enemies, and shapechange into centipedes, toads, or bats.

- **Str:** 5 (-3) | **Dex:** 17 (+3) | **Con:** 10 (+0) | **Int:** 7 (-2) | **Wis:** 10 (+0) | **Cha:** 10 (+0)
- **Damage Resistances:** Cold, fire, lightning; nonmagical physical damage
- **Damage Immunities:** Poison
- **Condition Immunities:** Poisoned
- **Traits:**
  - **Shapechanger:** Can polymorph into a bat, centipede, or toad.
  - **Magic Resistance:** Advantage on saving throws against spells and magical effects.
- **Actions:**
  - **Claws:** Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 5 (1d4 + 3) piercing damage plus 5 (2d4) poison damage (DC 10 Con save to negate and prevent Poisoned condition).
  - **Scare (1/Day):** One target within 20 ft. must succeed on a DC 10 Wisdom save or be Frightened for 1 minute.
  - **Invisibility:** Magically turns invisible until it attacks or concentration ends.
