# Beholder
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Aberration
> - Size:: Large
> - CR:: 13
> - AC:: 18 (natural)
> - HP:: 180 (19d10 + 76)
> - Speed:: 0 ft., Fly 20 ft. (hover)
> - Tags:: #monster #boss #legendary #aberration #underdark

A paranoid, xenophobic floating orb of flesh dominated by a central eye and ten eyestalks, each capable of firing lethal magical rays.

- **Str:** 10 (+0) | **Dex:** 14 (+2) | **Con:** 18 (+4) | **Int:** 17 (+3) | **Wis:** 15 (+2) | **Cha:** 17 (+3)
- **Senses:** Darkvision 120 ft.
- **Traits:**
  - **Antimagic Cone:** The central eye emits a 150-foot cone of antimagic, suppressing all spells, items, and magical effects within it.
- **Actions:**
  - **Bite:** Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 14 (4d6) piercing damage.
  - **Eye Rays:** Shoots three random eye rays at targets within 120 ft. (DC 16 saves): Charm, Paralyzing, Fear, Slowing, Enervation (36 necrotic), Telekinetic, Sleep, Petrification, Disintegration (45 force), or Death Ray (55 necrotic).
- **Legendary Actions (3/Round):** Can fire one random eye ray at the end of another creature's turn.
