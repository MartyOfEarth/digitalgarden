# Rust Monster
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Monstrosity
> - Size:: Medium
> - CR:: 1/2
> - AC:: 14 (natural)
> - HP:: 27 (5d8 + 5)
> - Speed:: 40 ft.
> - Tags:: #monster #dungeon-staple #monstrosity #underdark

Subterranean insectoid horrors that corrode and feast on ferrous metal, feared by armored [[Main/Classes/Paladin\|paladins]] and knights above almost all else.

- **Str:** 13 (+1) | **Dex:** 12 (+1) | **Con:** 13 (+1) | **Int:** 2 (-4) | **Wis:** 13 (+1) | **Cha:** 6 (-2)
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft.
- **Traits:**
  - **Iron Scent:** Accurately pinpoints the location of ferrous metal within 30 ft.
  - **Rust Metal:** Nonmagical weapons that hit the monster corrode (-1 penalty, destroyed at -5).
- **Actions:**
  - **Bite:** Melee Weapon Attack: +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 5 (1d8 + 1) piercing damage.
  - **Antennae:** Corrodes nonmagical armor or metal object touched within 5 ft. (permanent -1 penalty to armor AC until destroyed at -5).
