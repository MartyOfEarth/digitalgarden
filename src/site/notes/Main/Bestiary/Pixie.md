# Pixie
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Fey
> - Size:: Tiny
> - CR:: 1/4
> - AC:: 15
> - HP:: 1 (1d4 - 1)
> - Speed:: 10 ft., Fly 30 ft.
> - Tags:: #monster #fey #forest

Inquisitive, benevolent sprites of the Feywild that love harmless tricks, glittering [[Main/Races/Fairy\|fairy]] dust, and casting surprisingly potent spells.

- **Str:** 2 (-4) | **Dex:** 20 (+5) | **Con:** 8 (-1) | **Int:** 10 (+0) | **Wis:** 14 (+2) | **Cha:** 15 (+2)
- **Skills:** Perception +4, Stealth +7
- **Traits:**
  - **Magic Resistance:** Advantage on saves against spells and magical effects.
  - **Innate Spellcasting:** At will: *Druidcraft*. 1/day each: *Confusion*, *Dancing Lights*, *Detect Evil and Good*, *Detect Thoughts*, *Fly*, *Entangle*, *Phantasmal Force*, *Polymorph*, *Sleep*.
- **Actions:**
  - **Superior Invisibility:** Magically turns invisible. Concentration doesn't end if she casts a spell.
