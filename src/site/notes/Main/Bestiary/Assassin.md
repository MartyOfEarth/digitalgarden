# Assassin
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Humanoid
> - Size:: Medium
> - CR:: 8
> - AC:: 15 (studded leather)
> - HP:: 78 (12d8 + 24)
> - Speed:: 30 ft.
> - Tags:: #monster #npc #humanoid #urban

A ruthless contract killer specializing in stealth infiltration, lethal surprise strikes, and coating blades in wyvern poison.

- **Str:** 11 (+0) | **Dex:** 16 (+3) | **Con:** 14 (+2) | **Int:** 13 (+1) | **Wis:** 11 (+0) | **Cha:** 10 (+0)
- **Saving Throws:** Dex +6, Int +4
- **Skills:** Acrobatics +6, Deception +3, Perception +3, Stealth +9
- **Damage Resistances:** Poison
- **Traits:**
  - **Assassinate:** Advantage on attack rolls against creatures that haven't taken a turn yet; any hit against a surprised creature is an automatic critical hit.
  - **Sneak Attack:** Extra 14 (4d6) damage once per turn when attacking with advantage or with an ally within 5 ft.
- **Actions:**
  - **Multiattack:** Makes two shortsword attacks.
  - **Shortsword:** Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 6 (1d6 + 3) piercing damage plus 24 (7d6) poison damage (DC 15 Con save for half).
  - **Light Crossbow:** Ranged Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, range 80/320 ft., one target. Hit: 7 (1d8 + 3) piercing damage plus 24 (7d6) poison damage (DC 15 Con save for half).
