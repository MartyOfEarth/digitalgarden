# Mummy
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Undead
> - Size:: Medium
> - CR:: 3
> - AC:: 11 (natural)
> - HP:: 58 (9d8 + 18)
> - Speed:: 20 ft.
> - Tags:: #monster #dungeon-staple #undead #dungeon

Embalmed corpses bound in linen wrappings, guarding royal tombs and cursed pyramids, afflicting grave-robbers with deadly rot.

- **Str:** 16 (+3) | **Dex:** 8 (-1) | **Con:** 15 (+2) | **Int:** 6 (-2) | **Wis:** 10 (+0) | **Cha:** 12 (+1)
- **Damage Vulnerabilities:** Fire
- **Damage Resistances:** Nonmagical physical damage
- **Damage Immunities:** Necrotic, Poison
- **Condition Immunities:** Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Poisoned
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft.
- **Actions:**
  - **Dreadful Glare:** One target within 60 ft.; DC 11 Wis save or Frightened until end of mummy's next turn. If failed by 5+, target is paralyzed.
  - **Rotting Fist:** Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 10 (2d6 + 3) bludgeoning plus 10 (3d6) necrotic damage. Target must make a DC 12 Con save or be cursed with Mummy Rot (cannot regain HP; max HP drops by 10 every 24 hours).
