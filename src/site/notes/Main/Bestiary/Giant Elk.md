# Giant Elk
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Beast
> - Size:: Huge
> - CR:: 2
> - AC:: 14
> - HP:: 42 (5d12 + 10)
> - Speed:: 60 ft.
> - Capacity:: 1140 lbs
> - Tags:: #monster #mount #fey #forest

Majestic, intelligent monarchs of primeval forests. Giant elk understand [[Main/Languages/Sylvan\|Sylvan]] and Elvish, often serving fey monarchs, [[Main/Classes/Ranger\|ranger]] orders, and [[Main/Classes/Druid\|druids]] as noble mounts.

- **Str:** 19 (+4) | **Dex:** 16 (+3) | **Con:** 14 (+2) | **Int:** 7 (-2) | **Wis:** 14 (+2) | **Cha:** 10 (+0)
- **Traits:**
  - **Charge:** If the elk moves at least 20 ft. straight toward a target and hits with a ram attack, the target takes an extra 7 (2d6) damage and must make a DC 14 Strength save or fall prone.
- **Actions:**
  - **Ram:** Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 11 (2d6 + 4) bludgeoning damage.
  - **Hooves:** Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one prone target. Hit: 22 (4d8 + 4) bludgeoning damage.
