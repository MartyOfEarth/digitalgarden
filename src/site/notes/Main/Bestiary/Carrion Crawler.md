# Carrion Crawler
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Monstrosity
> - Size:: Large
> - CR:: 2
> - AC:: 13 (natural)
> - HP:: 51 (6d10 + 18)
> - Speed:: 30 ft., Climb 30 ft.
> - Tags:: #monster #dungeon-staple #monstrosity #underdark

A segmented, multi-legged subterranean scavenger with a ring of facial tentacles that paralyzes prey before consuming them whole.

- **Str:** 14 (+2) | **Dex:** 13 (+1) | **Con:** 16 (+3) | **Int:** 1 (-5) | **Wis:** 12 (+1) | **Cha:** 5 (-3)
- **Skills:** Perception +3
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft.
- **Traits:**
  - **Keen Smell:** Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on smell.
  - **Spider Climb:** Can climb difficult surfaces, including ceilings, without an ability check.
- **Actions:**
  - **Multiattack:** Makes two attacks: one with its tentacles and one with its bite.
  - **Tentacles:** Melee Weapon Attack: +8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one creature. Hit: 4 (1d4 + 2) poison damage, and target must make a DC 13 Con save or be Poisoned and Paralyzed for 1 minute.
  - **Bite:** Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 7 (2d4 + 2) piercing damage.
