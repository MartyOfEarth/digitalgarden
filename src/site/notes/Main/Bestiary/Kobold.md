Craven reptilian scavengers who dig deep mines, construct ingenious lethal traps, and worship chromatic dragons.

- **Str:** 7 (-2) | **Dex:** 15 (+2) | **Con:** 9 (-1) | **Int:** 8 (-1) | **Wis:** 7 (-2) | **Cha:** 8 (-1)
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft.
- **Traits:**
  - **Sunlight Sensitivity:** Disadvantage on attack rolls and Perception checks relying on sight in direct sunlight.
  - **Pack Tactics:** Advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one ally is within 5 ft. of the target.
- **Actions:**
  - **Dagger:** Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 4 (1d4 + 2) piercing damage.
  - **Sling:** Ranged Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, range 30/120 ft., one target. Hit: 4 (1d4 + 2) bludgeoning damage.
