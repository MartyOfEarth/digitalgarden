# Dire Wolf
> [!infobox] Statblock
> - Type:: Beast
> - Size:: Large
> - CR:: 1
> - AC:: 14 (natural)
> - HP:: 37 (5d10 + 10)
> - Speed:: 50 ft.
> - Tags:: #monster #summon #wildshape #forest #beast

An enormous prehistoric wolf that hunts in coordinated packs. Extremely popular for *Conjure Animals* thanks to Pack Tactics and automatic trip attacks on hit.

- **Str:** 17 (+3) | **Dex:** 15 (+2) | **Con:** 15 (+2) | **Int:** 3 (-4) | **Wis:** 12 (+1) | **Cha:** 7 (-2)
- **Skills:** Perception +3, Stealth +4
- **Traits:**
  - **Keen Hearing and Smell:** Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing or smell.
  - **Pack Tactics:** Advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one ally is within 5 ft. of the target and not incapacitated.
- **Actions:**
  - **Bite:** Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 10 (2d6 + 3) piercing damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 13 Strength save or be knocked prone.
