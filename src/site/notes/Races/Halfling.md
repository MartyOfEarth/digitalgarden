Cherished and guided by gods who value life, home, and hearth, [[Races/Halfling\|Halflings]] gravitate toward bucolic havens where family and community help shape their lives. That said, many [[Races/Halfling\|Halflings]] possess a brave and adventurous spirit that leads them on journeys of discovery, affording them the chance to explore a bigger world and make new friends along the way. Their size—similar to that of a [[Races/Human\|Human]] child—helps them pass through crowds unnoticed and slip through tight spaces.

Anyone who has spent time around [[Races/Halfling\|Halflings]], particularly [[Races/Halfling\|Halfling]] adventurers, has likely witnessed the storied “luck of the halflings” in action. When a [[Races/Halfling\|Halfling]] is in mortal danger, an unseen force seems to intervene on the [[Races/Halfling\|Halfling’s]] behalf. Many [[Races/Halfling\|Halflings]] believe in the power of luck, and they attribute their unusual gift to one or more of their benevolent gods, including Yondalla, Brandobaris, and Charmalaine. The same gift might contribute to their robust life spans (about 150 years).

[[Races/Halfling\|Halfling]] communities come in all varieties. For every sequestered shire tucked away in an unspoiled part of the world, there’s a crime syndicate like the Boromar Clan in the Eberron setting or a territorial mob of halflings like those in the Dark Sun setting.

[[Races/Halfling\|Halflings]] who prefer to live underground are sometimes called strongheart [[Races/Halfling\|Halflings]] or stouts. Nomadic [[Races/Halfling\|Halflings]], as well as those who live among [[Races/Human\|Humans]] and other tall folk, are sometimes called lightfoot [[Races/Halfling\|Halflings]] or tallfellows.
